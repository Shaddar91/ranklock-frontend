Haze was picked 2,174,446 times between 2026-05-01 and 2026-09-03, more than
any other hero on the 38-hero roster, and won 52.5% of those games. That number
lands exactly on the S cut the [tier list](/tier-list/) uses, so the hero sits
in S with no room to spare. The newest match in the set started at
2026-09-02T23:27:36Z.

## Haze by the numbers

The [Haze hero page](/heroes/haze/) reads 9.1 kills, 7.1 deaths and 10.8
assists per game across that window, with 44,867 souls of average net worth
and an average match length of 37:27. Nine kills a game is the highest kill
figure on the roster, and it says what a team gets from the pick: a gun carry
that turns farm into kills from the mid game onward. Sleep Dagger and Smoke
Bomb set up the engagement, Fixation stacks the damage, and Bullet Dance is the
fight-ending button once the items are in. Everything in the item table below
follows from that shape. Haze wants gun items, she wants them early, and the
win rates reward the players who reach the expensive ones.

## The items that win on Haze

Four items lead Haze's table by win rate across the games each was bought in,
2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03, counting only items with at least 174,926 Haze games
behind them (2% of the hero's largest item sample). [Lucky
Shot](/items/1396247347/) wins 62.2% of 1,456,555 games. Frenzy wins 62.0% of
432,983. Glass Cannon wins 61.8% of 843,525. Spiritual Overflow wins 61.3% of
1,808,014. Below those sit Healing Tempo at 60.7% over 354,594 games and
Silencer at 60.6% over 3,628,841.

Three of the top four are late gun items and the fourth, Spiritual Overflow,
is a spirit item that still pays on her. That is the build direction the data
gives you: get to the expensive gun slots, and get there with the game still
open. One caution belongs next to every figure in this section. An item bought
at 30 minutes is bought in games that lasted 30 minutes, usually by the side
that could afford it, so a late item's win rate carries some of the lead that
paid for it. The 62.2% on Lucky Shot is real over 1.4 million games; it is
also partly a description of Haze players who were already ahead.

## What most Haze players actually buy

The buy list and the win list are different lists. Sorted by games, Haze's four
most-bought items in the window are Swift Striker at 8,746,317 games and
51.1%, Extra Spirit at 8,193,019 and 51.0%, Burst Fire at 8,011,453 and 52.2%,
and Rapid Rounds at 8,006,757 and 51.0%. Those are laning items, bought in
nearly every game, and their win rates sit within two points of the hero's own
52.5%. They do not decide games. Lucky Shot, Frenzy and Glass Cannon, the items
at the top of the win-rate list, sit far down the buy list, which is the
common mistake in one sentence: Haze players spend their first souls the same
way and then part ways on the buys that matter.

The bottom of the table is worth a look too. Cultist Sacrifice wins 48.0% of
the 221,287 Haze games it appears in and Spirit Shielding wins 48.5% of
366,002. Both are more than four points under the hero's baseline. If either
is in your default build, that is the slot to reconsider first.

## The four matchups worth preparing

Sorting Haze's matchup table by raw win rate names Sinclair at 58.3% as the
best lane and Victor at 47.0% as the worst, and both readings mislead, because
they mostly repeat that Sinclair is a weak hero and Victor a strong one.
RankLock's build page instead compares each row to what the two heroes'
overall win rates predict: 50% plus Haze's rate minus the opponent's. Summed
across the five rank brackets for the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window, 37
opponents clear the sample floor of 9,736 matches.

By that gap, Haze does best into [Graves](/heroes/graves/), winning 49.0% of
397,629 matches where 47.3% was predicted, 1.7 points over, and into Celeste,
55.1% against a predicted 53.7% over 317,287 matches. The two lanes with the
least room are [Calico](/heroes/calico/), 50.9% against a predicted 51.7% over
242,184 matches, and Pocket, 54.6% against 55.3% over 239,028. Calico and
Pocket belong together as a pair; Pocket sits within two hundredths of a point
of two other heroes, so the order between them is rounding.

None of those four gaps reaches two points, so none of them is a counter-pick
and none of them should change your pick. What they change is the plan. Into
Graves and Celeste the lane gives more than the ratings say it should, so
playing for the early kill pays. Into Calico and Pocket, both lanes where you
still win more than half the games, the data says to take the farm and let the
late items do the arguing.

## Haze gets worse as you climb

Filter the [heroes grid](/heroes/) by rank band and Haze's win rate slides
almost the whole way up the ladder. It reads 53.7% at Initiate, peaks at 53.9%
at Alchemist, then falls through 53.2% at Emissary, 52.6% at Archon, 52.1% at
Oracle, 51.2% at Phantom and 50.2% at Ascendant to 49.4% at Eternus, all in
the same 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window. That is a 4.5-point drop from peak
to top, the largest of the four most-picked heroes.

Run the tier cuts on those numbers and Haze is an S hero at Alchemist, an A
hero at Phantom, a B hero at Ascendant and a C hero at Eternus. A player at the
top of the ladder picking Haze off the all-ranks 52.5% is picking a different
hero than the one the number describes. The table does not say why, only that
it happens in every band above Archon, and no item choice reverses it. The
[rank band guide](/blog/rank-bands-hero-choice/) covers how to read your own
band's row before you lock in.

## What the community publishes, phase by phase

Three published Haze builds reach RankLock's list from the public build feed.
Two of them are versions 34 and 27 of the same build by the same author,
Account 1183614423, at 17,027 and 16,147 weekly favorites; the third is
"ww dkdion" by Account 120623629 at 11,100. The top build's name carries a
streaming handle, so it is cited here by author and favorite count.

Its early block is Active Reload, Rapid Rounds, Swift Striker, Extra Health,
Extra Spirit, Surge of Power, Extra Stamina and Kinetic Dash, which is the
most-bought list from above with a few additions. Its mid block is Fortitude,
Slowing Hex, Bullet Lifesteal, Burst Fire, Ricochet, Lucky Shot and Vampiric
Burst. Ricochet wins 55.0% of 5,825,883 Haze games and Lucky Shot 62.2%, so
the mid game is where this build starts beating the baseline. Its late block
is Glass Cannon, Armor Piercing Rounds, Crippling Headshot, Cursed Relic and
Capacitor, measured at 61.8%, 58.3% over 545,409 games, 59.9% over 2,179,715,
54.6% over 719,934 and 55.0% over 2,706,118.

Set that against the win-rate table and two gaps show. Frenzy, 62.0% in the
data, is in the build's optional list rather than its core, and Spiritual
Overflow, 61.3% over 1.8 million games, does not appear in version 34 at all.
Whether those two belong in your late block is the decision this post cannot
make for you, and the [Haze build page](/heroes/haze/build/) is where the
measured sets, the buy order and all three published builds sit side by side
to make it with.
