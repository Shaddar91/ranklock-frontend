Spend your first souls the way every other Haze does, then part ways at the
slots that decide games. [Lucky Shot](/items/1396247347/) wins 62.2% of the
1,456,555 Haze games it was bought in, Frenzy 62.0% of 432,983 and Glass
Cannon 61.8% of 843,525, against the 52.5% she wins across 2,174,446 games in
the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window. All three sit far down the buy list, and
getting to them with the game still open is the build.

## Keep shooting the same body

Hold your fire on one target until it drops. Fixation stacks damage on
whatever keeps taking your bullets, so swapping to finish a low enemy throws
away the mechanic the hero is built on, and Sleep Dagger, Smoke Bomb and
Bullet Dance all exist to buy the seconds those stacks need. The [Haze hero
page](/heroes/haze/) reads 9.1 kills, 7.1 deaths and 10.8 assists a game
across the window, with 44,867 souls of average net worth and an average match
of 37:27. Nine kills a game is the highest figure on the roster and it arrives
from the mid game onward, not out of lane. Sequencing the abilities is the
[Haze guide](/heroes/haze/guide/); what you are sequencing toward is the item
table.

## The items worth reaching

Get to the expensive gun slots before the game closes. Counting only items
with at least 174,926 Haze games behind them, 2% of her largest item sample,
four lead the table in the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window: Lucky Shot at
62.2% over 1,456,555 games, Frenzy at 62.0% over 432,983, Glass Cannon at
61.8% over 843,525 and Spiritual Overflow at 61.3% over 1,808,014. Healing
Tempo sits under them at 60.7% over 354,594 games and Silencer at 60.6% over
3,628,841.

Three of the top four are late gun items and the fourth is a spirit item that
still pays on her, so the direction is not in doubt. Take one caution with
every figure in that list. An item bought at thirty minutes is bought in games
that lasted thirty minutes, usually by the side that could afford it, so a
late item's rate carries some of the lead that paid for it. Lucky Shot's 62.2%
over 1.4 million games is real, and it also describes Haze players who were
already ahead.

## Change the second half of your build, not the first

The buy list and the win list are different lists, and only one of them is
worth editing. Sorted by games, Haze's four most-bought items in the window
are Swift Striker at 8,746,317 games and 51.1%, Extra Spirit at 8,193,019 and
51.0%, Burst Fire at 8,011,453 and 52.2%, and Rapid Rounds at 8,006,757 and
51.0%. Those are laning items, bought in nearly every game, and every one sits
within two points of the hero's own 52.5%. They decide nothing. The items at
the top of the win-rate list sit far down that buy list, which is the whole
gap in one sentence: Haze players spend their early souls identically and then
part ways on the buys that matter.

Two habits are worth deleting outright. Cultist Sacrifice wins 48.0% of the
221,287 Haze games it appears in and Spirit Shielding 48.5% of 366,002, both
more than four points under the baseline. If either is in your default build,
that is the slot to reconsider first.

## Which lanes pay for the early kill

Play the lane for tempo, not for the draft. Sorting Haze's matchup table by
raw win rate names Sinclair at 58.3% as her best lane and Victor at 47.0% as
her worst, and both readings mislead, because they mostly repeat that Sinclair
is a weak hero and Victor a strong one. RankLock's build page instead compares
each row to what the two overall win rates predict, 50% plus Haze's rate minus
the opponent's. Summed across the five rank brackets for the 2026-05-01 to
2026-09-03 window, 37 opponents clear the sample floor of 9,736 matches.

By that gap she does best into [Graves](/heroes/graves/), winning 49.0% of
397,629 matches where 47.3% was predicted, and into Celeste, 55.1% against a
predicted 53.7% over 317,287 matches. The two lanes with the least room are
[Calico](/heroes/calico/), 50.9% against a predicted 51.7% over 242,184
matches, and Pocket, 54.6% against 55.3% over 239,028. Pocket sits within two
hundredths of a point of two other heroes, so read it and Calico as a pair
rather than a ranking.

None of those four gaps reaches two points, so none of them should change your
pick. They change the plan. Into Graves and Celeste the lane gives more than
the ratings say it should, so playing for the early kill pays. Into Calico and
Pocket, both lanes you still win more than half of, take the farm and let the
late items do the arguing.

## She gets worse as you climb

Check your own band's row before you lock her in, because her number moves
further than most. Filtered by band on the [heroes grid](/heroes/), Haze reads
53.7% at Initiate, peaks at 53.9% at Alchemist, then falls through 53.2% at
Emissary, 52.6% at Archon, 52.1% at Oracle, 51.2% at Phantom and 50.2% at
Ascendant to 49.4% at Eternus, all in the same window. That is a 4.5-point
drop from peak to top, the largest of the four most-picked heroes.

Run the tier cuts on those rows and she is an S hero at Alchemist, an A hero
at Phantom, a B hero at Ascendant and a C hero at Eternus. A player at the top
of the ladder picking her off the all-ranks 52.5% is picking a different hero
than the number describes. The table does not say why, only that it happens in
every band above Archon, and no item choice reverses it. The [rank band
guide](/blog/rank-bands-hero-choice/) covers reading your own row before you
lock in.

## What the published builds leave out

Take the order from the published lists and the purchases from the table,
because the two part company at the end. Three published Haze builds reach
RankLock's list from the public build feed. Two of them are versions 34 and 27
of the same build by the same author, Account 1183614423, at 17,027 and 16,147
weekly favorites; the third is "ww dkdion" by Account 120623629 at 11,100. The
top build's name carries a streaming handle, so it is cited here by author and
favorite count.

Its early block is Active Reload, Rapid Rounds, Swift Striker, Extra Health,
Extra Spirit, Surge of Power, Extra Stamina and Kinetic Dash, which is the
most-bought list from above with a few additions. Its mid block is Fortitude,
Slowing Hex, Bullet Lifesteal, Burst Fire, Ricochet, Lucky Shot and Vampiric
Burst. That block is where the build starts beating the baseline, with
Ricochet at 55.0% over 5,825,883 Haze games and Lucky Shot at 62.2%. Its late
block is Glass Cannon, Armor Piercing Rounds, Crippling Headshot, Cursed Relic
and Capacitor, measured at 61.8%, 58.3% over 545,409 games, 59.9% over
2,179,715, 54.6% over 719,934 and 55.0% over 2,706,118.

Two of the winning items are missing from it. Frenzy, 62.0% in the data, sits
in the build's optional list rather than its core, and Spiritual Overflow,
61.3% over 1.8 million games, does not appear in version 34 at all. Whether
those two earn a late slot on your build is the decision this post cannot make
for you, and the [Haze build page](/heroes/haze/build/) is where the measured
sets, the buy order and all three published builds sit side by side to make it
with.
