Item win rates on RankLock split into five brackets, and the split lives in
two places: the [items page](/items/) itself, and the "Best items at your
rank" table on every build page. The winning item sets shown higher up a
build page stay all-ranks only; the rank-sliced numbers live in that one
table. The five brackets read All ranks, Initiate-Alchemist,
Arcanist-Ritualist, Emissary-Archon, Oracle-Phantom, and Ascendant-Eternus.
See it filtered on [Haze's build page](/heroes/haze/build/), where clicking
a different bracket button rebuilds the whole table in place.

## The top bracket is a sliver of the data

Summed across all 156 tracked items, the current window holds 287,938,047
buy records at all ranks, 33,511,017 at Initiate-Alchemist, 22,348,804 at
Emissary-Archon, and 746,327 at Ascendant-Eternus. The top bracket holds
roughly one buy record in every 386. Spread those 746,327 records across 156
items and the average item sees under 5,000 buys at the top bracket, before
accounting for the items far more or less popular than average. Every rate
quoted from that bracket below is measured against a sample two orders of
magnitude smaller than the all-ranks figure sitting next to it.

## What a small bracket does to a win rate

[Glass Cannon](/items/365620721/) reads 56.38% over 476,959 matches at all
ranks and 64.52% over 217 matches at Ascendant-Eternus, a jump of more than
eight points on a sample that shrank by a factor of roughly 2,200.
[Metal Skin](/items/1378931225/) moves the same direction: 40.15% over
534,479 matches at all ranks against 45.97% over 1,240 at the top bracket.
Neither jump is evidence that either item plays better at high rank. A rate
built from a few hundred matches swings hard in both directions, and only
one direction, the one that flatters the item, tends to get repeated.

The swing runs the other way just as easily. Leech rises further than Glass
Cannon does, 54.18% over 1,175,805 matches at all ranks to 64.53% over 516
at the top bracket, a gain over ten points on a sample that at least clears
five hundred. Magic Carpet falls the opposite direction just as hard: 56.94%
over 184,355 matches at all ranks against 41.18% over only 136 at
Ascendant-Eternus, a drop of nearly sixteen points. Nobody writes a post
titled "Magic Carpet gets worse at high rank" from that number, because a
136-match sample earns no headline either way. The thin bracket doesn't
favor good news; it just amplifies whatever the small sample happened to
run into that week.

## The same trap, hero-scoped

Filter [Haze's](/heroes/haze/) item table to Ascendant-Eternus and only 71 of
the 156 tracked items have any rows at all, totaling 14,904 buy records
against 9,140,489 at all ranks. The nominal leader in that thin slice right
now is Duration Extender at 75.0% over 20 matches, a count too small to
trust before the next patch even lands. The number worth acting on sits in
the all-ranks table instead: Lucky Shot at 62.18% over 89,919 matches, a
sample large enough to hold its shape.

## The brackets worth trusting, and the field nobody reads

Initiate-Alchemist and Emissary-Archon carry enough volume, 33,511,017 and
22,348,804 buy records, to separate real behavior from noise, and the two
brackets do disagree on real items. Crippling Headshot reads 55.79% at
Initiate-Alchemist and 56.68% at Emissary-Archon; Ricochet reads 52.21% and
52.29%. Filter to whichever of the first four brackets covers your own rank
and the numbers hold up.

Even inside the thin top bracket, not every item is equally thin. Crippling
Headshot still carries 2,833 matches at Ascendant-Eternus and Ricochet
carries 3,145, both more than ten times Glass Cannon's 217 in the same
bracket, because Crippling Headshot and Ricochet simply get bought more
often across every rank. A count in the low thousands at the top bracket is
worth reading; a count in the low hundreds is the one to hold at arm's
length. The bracket a row sits in never tells you that on its own, only the
count next to it does.

Buy time is the column most players never open, and it survives a thin
bracket better than a win rate does, because timing needs a smaller sample
than a rate to mean something. Improved Spirit is bought at 14.0 minutes on
average, 36.7% of the way into the match; Juggernaut at 36.8 minutes, 82.6%
in. Glass Cannon's timing compresses as the bracket rises: 31.6 minutes at
Initiate-Alchemist against 27.4 minutes at Ascendant-Eternus, the one place
the thin top bracket still says something a reader can use. See the full
curve on [Improved Spirit's](/items/7409189/) and
[Juggernaut's](/items/1250307611/) own pages.

## The rule

Read the match count before the rate. Prefer the bracket you actually play
in, as long as it clears a few thousand matches. Treat any row under that
floor as a question rather than an answer, especially at the top bracket,
where a single lucky week of pub games can carry a win rate eight points in
either direction, and just as easily carry one sixteen points the other way.
None of that makes the top bracket useless, only narrow: it is still the
only place on the site that shows what Ascendant and Eternus players
actually bought, and a narrow window beats no window at all as long as the
count riding next to it gets read first. [Hero matchups 101](/blog/hero-matchups-101/)
applies the same count-before-rate discipline to matchup rows, and the
[methodology page](/methodology/) documents how every bracket gets built.
