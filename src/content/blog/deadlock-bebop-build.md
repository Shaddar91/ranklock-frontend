Bebop was picked 2,067,938 times in the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window,
second on the roster behind Haze, and won 46.5% of those games. The [tier
list](/tier-list/) grades anything under 47.0% as F, so the second most-picked
hero in the data is also the only one of the four most-picked heroes in the
bottom grade.

## The most-picked losing hero

The gap between pick volume and win rate is the whole story of the hero, and
the [Bebop hero page](/heroes/bebop/) shows where the losses come from. Bebop
averages 7.1 kills, 8.1 deaths and 13.8 assists a game across the window, with
41,323 souls of average net worth, the lowest of the four most-picked heroes,
and an average match of 37:28. The 13.8 assists are the highest assist count
in that group, nearly two for every kill. Bebop's job for a team is to make
kills happen rather than to take them. Grapple Arm drags one enemy out of
position, Sticky Bomb and Exploding Uppercut punish whoever got pulled, and
Hyper Beam ends fights the team has already tilted. A hero built to set up
other people's kills dies more than the carries it feeds, which is where the
8.1 deaths come from, and the item table has to be read with that in mind.

## The item lines that hold up

Counting only items with at least 174,325 Bebop games behind them, 2% of the
hero's largest item sample, five items in the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window
beat Bebop's own 46.5% by eight points or more. [Mystic
Reverb](/items/3577481646/) wins 56.3% of 3,542,289 games. Silencer wins
56.1% of 395,315. [Spirit Burn](/items/343572757/) wins 55.4% of 2,396,712.
Siphon Bullets wins 55.0% of 980,106. Witchmail wins 54.5% of 2,849,072.

Three of the five are spirit items, and the samples are large enough that the
ordering between them is real. A gap of nearly ten points over the hero's
baseline on 3.5 million games is the clearest signal in any table in this
batch. It says the Bebop games that go long enough to afford Mystic Reverb and
Spirit Burn are the Bebop games that get won, and a player deciding how to
spend the souls after laning has the direction handed to them.

## The three items to stop buying

A losing hero loses more at the bottom of its table than a winning one, so the
bottom rows matter more here than on Haze or Seven. Disarming Hex wins 36.8%
of the 323,401 Bebop games it appears in. [Metal Skin](/items/1378931225/)
wins 38.1% of 523,064. Rusted Barrel wins 39.0% of 207,780. Knockdown, one
row up, wins 41.3% of 716,441.

Metal Skin is the clearest of those. On the [items page](/items/) it wins
40.1% of 9,265,427 games across every hero, and on Bebop it does two points
worse than that. It was bought in 523,064 Bebop games in the window and lost
61.9% of them. Some of that is selection, because a Bebop who needs Metal Skin
is already being shot, but a two-point gap under an item's own roster-wide
rate is the mistake this hero makes most often. Spending souls to survive a
fight the team was losing anyway is the buy the data cannot justify, and
Disarming Hex sits in the same place for the same reason.

## The matchups, and what the residual actually says

Bebop's worst raw matchup row is Seven at 40.4% over 372,839 matches, and that
row is nearly useless as advice, because Seven wins 55.9% of everything. To
separate a hard lane from a strong opponent, RankLock's build page compares
each row to what the two win rates predict, 50% plus Bebop's rate minus the
opponent's, over the five rank brackets summed for the 2026-05-01 to
2026-09-03 window, with a floor of 8,510 matches that 37 opponents clear.

Read that way, Bebop's best result is into [Graves](/heroes/graves/), 42.6%
where 41.3% was predicted, 1.3 points over across 339,721 matches. A 42.6% row
is still a losing lane; it is the lane Bebop loses least badly relative to the
two ratings, and that is a different claim. Mo & Krill is the other positive
row, 46.3% against a predicted 45.4% over 247,608 matches. The lanes with the
least room are [Mirage](/heroes/mirage/), 48.0% against a predicted 49.2% over
186,645 matches, and Silver, 49.1% against 50.0% over 251,185. Mina sits at
the same rounded gap as Silver, two hundredths of a point apart, so treat
Silver and Mina as one answer rather than a ranked pair.

Every gap on the hero is under 1.3 points, so none of these lanes is a
counter-pick either way. What the rows change is how early you look for the
Grapple Arm pull. Against Graves the lane pays for aggression the ratings did
not expect; against Mirage and Silver, two matchups where Bebop wins close to
half the games anyway, the numbers favour farming toward Mystic Reverb over
forcing an early pull.

## Where Bebop's win rate goes on the ladder

Bebop's band curve is U-shaped. On the [heroes grid](/heroes/) filtered by
band, it wins 49.1% at Initiate, 47.0% at Seeker, 46.5% at Alchemist, 46.1% at
Arcanist, 45.7% at Ritualist, 45.8% at Emissary and 45.6% at Archon, the
bottom of the curve. Then it recovers, 45.7% at Oracle, 46.4% at Phantom,
46.8% at Ascendant and 48.2% at Eternus, all in the same window.

The hero is at its worst in the middle of the ladder rather than at the
bottom. Under the tier cuts that makes Bebop a C hero at Initiate, a D hero at
Seeker, an F hero from Alchemist through Ascendant, and a C hero again at
Eternus. The [rank band guide](/blog/rank-bands-hero-choice/) covers reading
your own band's row before you pick, and for Bebop it matters more than for
most heroes: the all-ranks 46.5% overstates the hero for an Archon player and
understates it at either end.

## Eight published builds, by phase

Eight published Bebop builds reach RankLock's list, more than any other hero
in the top four. The most favorited, at 14,841 weekly favorites by Account
1206811165, carries a name that does not read well under ad review, so it is
cited here by its author and its items. "w bebop(puzo)" by Account 1681161871
follows at 12,038, and "REVERSE BEBOP V2" by Account 1893991285, a gun-leaning
build at 5,502, is the outlier in a list of spirit builds.

The top build's laning block is Mystic Burst, Sprint Boots, Extra Spirit,
Trophy Collector, Improved Spirit, Mystic Expansion, Compress Cooldown and
Extra Stamina. Mystic Burst is the most-bought item on the hero at 8,716,260
games and wins 46.5% of them, exactly the hero's baseline, which is what a
laning item should do. Its post-lane block is Echo Shard, Mystic Reverb,
Witchmail, Boundless Spirit, Surge of Power, Superior Cooldown, Greater
Expansion and Tankbuster, and its late block is Spirit Burn, Transcendent
Cooldown and Focus Lens. Two of the winning lines, Mystic Reverb and Witchmail,
sit in the mid game and the third, Spirit Burn, closes the build, so the
published build and the measured table agree on the spine.

They disagree around it. Surge of Power wins 47.2% of 4,061,365 Bebop games,
Tankbuster 47.0% of 4,935,300 and Echo Shard 48.9% of 4,305,966, all within
two and a half points of the baseline, and the same build keeps Metal Skin and
Disarming Hex in its situational lists. The "w bebop(puzo)" build goes Echo
Shard and Boundless Spirit straight out of lane and closes on Infuser and
Spirit Burn. The [Bebop build page](/heroes/bebop/build/) lays all eight next
to the measured item sets and the buy order, which is the place to decide
which of the mid-game slots are yours.
