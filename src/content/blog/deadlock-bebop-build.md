Put your souls into spirit scaling and let the pull pay for it. [Mystic
Reverb](/items/3577481646/) wins 56.3% of the 3,542,289 Bebop games it was
bought in and [Spirit Burn](/items/343572757/) 55.4% of 2,396,712, on a hero
who wins 46.5% of his 2,067,938 games in the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window.
Nearly ten points of separation on samples that size is the clearest direction
any of the four most-picked heroes hands a player, and it is a spending plan
rather than an argument about the pick.

## What the hook is buying you

Play for the pull you can land now instead of the better one later. Bebop's
line on the [Bebop hero page](/heroes/bebop/) reads 7.1 kills, 8.1 deaths and
13.8 assists a game across the window, 41,323 souls of average net worth and
an average match of 37:28. Nearly two assists for every kill is the shape of a
hero paid to make other people's kills happen: Grapple Arm drags one enemy out
of position, Sticky Bomb and Exploding Uppercut punish whoever got pulled, and
Hyper Beam ends a fight the team has already tipped. The deaths come with that
job and they are not a reason to buy armour, which is the mistake the item
table catches most often. Where to hook from is the [Bebop
guide](/heroes/bebop/guide/); what to spend on once it lands is the rest of
this post.

## Buy the spirit line first

Count only the items with enough Bebop games behind them to argue with, at
least 174,325, or 2% of the hero's largest item sample. Five clear the 46.5%
baseline by eight points or more in the 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-03 window.
Mystic Reverb leads at 56.3% over 3,542,289 games, Silencer follows at 56.1%
over 395,315, Spirit Burn at 55.4% over 2,396,712, Siphon Bullets at 55.0%
over 980,106 and Witchmail at 54.5% over 2,849,072.

Three of the five are spirit items and the samples are large enough that the
order between them is real. The Bebop games that run long enough to afford
Mystic Reverb and Spirit Burn are the Bebop games that get won, so treat every
soul after laning as a route to those two slots and let Witchmail fill the
third. Most of what is left after that is preference.

## Stop buying Metal Skin

Cut the defensive buys before you argue about late slots, because a losing
hero loses more at the bottom of its table than a winning one does. Disarming
Hex wins 36.8% of the 323,401 Bebop games it appears in. [Metal
Skin](/items/1378931225/) wins 38.1% of 523,064. Rusted Barrel wins 39.0% of
207,780, and Knockdown one row up wins 41.3% of 716,441.

Metal Skin is the clearest of the four. Across every hero on the [items
page](/items/) it wins 40.1% of 9,265,427 games, and on Bebop it does two
points worse than its own roster rate. Some of that is selection, since a
Bebop who needs Metal Skin is already being shot, but two points under an
item's own average is the buy to drop first. Souls spent surviving a fight the
team was losing anyway are souls that never reached Mystic Reverb, and
Disarming Hex sits in the same place for the same reason.

## When to go hunting for the early pull

Read the matchup rows for pace rather than for a pick. Bebop's worst raw row
is Seven at 40.4% over 372,839 matches and it tells you almost nothing,
because Seven wins 55.9% of everything. RankLock's build page compares each
row to what the two win rates predict, 50% plus Bebop's rate minus the
opponent's, summed over the five rank brackets for the 2026-05-01 to
2026-09-03 window, with a floor of 8,510 matches that 37 opponents clear.

By that measure his best result is into [Graves](/heroes/graves/), 42.6% where
41.3% was predicted, 1.3 points over across 339,721 matches. It is still a
losing lane, and it is the lane he loses least badly for his rating. Mo &
Krill is the other positive row at 46.3% against a predicted 45.4% over
247,608 matches. The tightest rows are [Mirage](/heroes/mirage/) at 48.0%
against a predicted 49.2% over 186,645 matches and Silver at 49.1% against
50.0% over 251,185. Mina lands two hundredths of a point from Silver, so read
those two as one answer rather than a ranked pair.

Every gap sits under 1.3 points, so none of these lanes is a counter-pick and
none of them changes who you take. They change when you start looking for the
Grapple Arm pull. Into Graves the lane pays for aggression the two ratings did
not price in. Into Mirage and Silver, where Bebop wins close to half his games
anyway, farm toward Mystic Reverb instead of forcing the hook.

## Check your own band before you pick him

Look up your band's row first, because the all-ranks figure describes almost
nobody. Filtered by band on the [heroes grid](/heroes/), Bebop wins 49.1% at
Initiate, 47.0% at Seeker, 46.5% at Alchemist, 46.1% at Arcanist, 45.7% at
Ritualist, 45.8% at Emissary and 45.6% at Archon, the bottom of the curve,
then recovers through 45.7% at Oracle, 46.4% at Phantom and 46.8% at Ascendant
to 48.2% at Eternus, all in the same window.

The curve is U-shaped, and the middle of the ladder is where the hero is
worst. Under the tier cuts that makes him a C hero at Initiate, a D hero at
Seeker, an F hero from Alchemist through Ascendant and a C hero again at
Eternus. An Archon player picking off the all-ranks 46.5% is over-rating him
and an Eternus player is under-rating him. The [rank band
guide](/blog/rank-bands-hero-choice/) covers the habit worth building.

## What the published builds agree on

Eight published Bebop builds reach RankLock's list, more than any hero in the
top four, and they agree about the spine. The most favorited, at 14,841 weekly
favorites by Account 1206811165, carries a name that does not read well under
ad review, so it is cited here by its author and its items. "w bebop(puzo)" by
Account 1681161871 follows at 12,038, and "REVERSE BEBOP V2" by Account
1893991285 at 5,502 is the one gun build in a list of spirit builds.

Lane on the top build's block and you buy Mystic Burst, Sprint Boots, Extra
Spirit, Trophy Collector, Improved Spirit, Mystic Expansion, Compress Cooldown
and Extra Stamina. Mystic Burst is the most-bought item on the hero at
8,716,260 games and wins 46.5% of them, exactly the baseline, which is what a
laning item should do. Out of lane the same build goes Echo Shard, Mystic
Reverb, Witchmail, Boundless Spirit, Surge of Power, Superior Cooldown,
Greater Expansion and Tankbuster, then closes on Spirit Burn, Transcendent
Cooldown and Focus Lens. Two of the three winning lines sit in its mid game
and the third ends it.

The disagreements are around that spine, and they are where your own slots
are. Surge of Power wins 47.2% of 4,061,365 Bebop games, Tankbuster 47.0% of
4,935,300 and Echo Shard 48.9% of 4,305,966, all inside two and a half points
of the baseline, and the same build keeps Metal Skin and Disarming Hex in its
situational lists. The "w bebop(puzo)" list takes Echo Shard and Boundless
Spirit straight out of lane and closes on Infuser and Spirit Burn. The [Bebop
build page](/heroes/bebop/build/) sets all eight beside the measured item sets
and the buy order, which is where to settle which mid-game slots are yours.
