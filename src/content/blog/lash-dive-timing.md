Lash lives and dies by timing. Land the dive a beat early and the enemy
support peels; land it a beat late and the wave has already crashed. This guide
breaks down the single decision that separates a winning Lash from a feeding one:
**when to commit the slam.**

The chips below pull RankLock's measured win-rates for the current patch, so the
numbers in this guide never go stale.

## Read the cooldowns, not the health bars

The mistake most players make is diving on low health. Health regenerates; a used
escape ability does not. Before you slam, ask one question: **has the target
spent their movement cooldown in the last six seconds?** If yes, the dive lands.
If no, you are gambling.

- Hold high ground until the enemy throws a wave-clear ability.
- Track the support's cooldown — that is the peel that kills you.
- Slam toward your team, never away from it.

## The item spike that makes it work

Lash's dive becomes reliable the moment his first major item comes online. Rush
your power spike, then look for the fight. Pairing burst with a survivability
component lets you commit without instantly dying to focus fire.

## Matchups that flip the timing

Against mobile carries the window shrinks — you dive *reactively*, after they
commit. Against grounded heroes you dive *proactively*, opening the fight on your
terms. The hero detail page tracks these matchups per bracket as the data fills
in.

Practice the read in your next ten games: count the cooldown, then commit. The
win-rate follows.
