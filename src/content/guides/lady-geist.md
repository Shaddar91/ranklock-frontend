## Who plays Lady Geist, and why

Pick Lady Geist when you are willing to spend your own health bar as ammunition, and plan on ending the match before the enemy carries arrive. Her curve falls hard: 55.1% win rate under 30 minutes, 50.5% from 30 to 45 and 48.3% past 45, a 6.8-point drop from start to finish. She has 880 HP and 3 stamina at 6.3 m/s, and every one of those numbers is there to be traded.

Two of her abilities cost health to cast, and that is the whole design. Essence Bomb spends health to lob a bomb that arms and then detonates. Life Drain tethers an enemy and pulls their health into hers, and the alternate cast sends the heal to an ally instead. Malice spends health for shards that slow and amplify her damage against whatever they stick to. Soul Exchange swaps her health total with an enemy hero's.

## How to play Lady Geist as an Offensive initiator

Open the fight at low health on purpose. Two abilities charge you health, one gives it back, and the ultimate hands whatever is left to somebody else, so the sequence that reads as reckless is the one the kit was built around. Bomb, shard, tether — and if it goes badly, swap.

Soul Exchange is a finisher and an escape in one button, and picking which it is today is the only genuinely hard decision on the hero. Swapping onto a full-health enemy while you are nearly dead trades your problem for theirs. Swapping onto one who is already low hands them your bar and your fight, so read the target's health before the tether ends, not after.

Choose the lanes where the opening is free. She wins 56.5% of 113,998 matches against Sinclair, 55.9% of 336,447 against Venator, 55.7% of 306,039 against Mina, 54.9% of 309,140 against Bebop and 54.3% of 225,909 against Silver. Those are the games to close early.

## How to play Lady Geist as a Damage carry

Put Malice on the target before the bomb lands, never after. The shards slow and amplify her damage on whatever they hit, so the order decides whether Essence Bomb detonates into an amplified body or a plain one. That is the whole gap between a trade and a kill, and it costs nothing but sequencing.

Buy the amplifiers with the biggest tables behind them. Spirit Burn wins 56.7% of 2,189,500 games on her, Escalating Exposure 55.4% of 2,869,071 and Boundless Spirit 54.5% of 2,114,923 — three of the largest samples on the hero, all pointing the same way. Mystic Reverb reads 55.7% over 1,912,729.

Lucky Shot shows 56.4% across only 11,769 games, the smallest sample in her top ten, so it is a lead rather than a rule. The gun half of the build is real regardless: Frenzy wins 54.9% of 420,445 games and Mercurial Magnum 54.5% of 205,966.

## The build on the 2026-08-22 Update patch

This patch hit the item she leans on hardest. Radiant Regeneration lost heal on cast from 70 to 65, and it sits in 27 of the 40 sampled Lady Geist builds — more than two-thirds of them — while her players already buy it 6.3 times more often than the roster average. Restorative Locket also dropped from 10% Spirit Resistance to 8%, but that one appears in 2 builds and changes nothing.

The answer is a smaller sustain package, not a different one. Mystic Regeneration is her other signature purchase at 7.8 times the roster average and it was not touched, so the health you spend still comes back; it comes back slightly slower per cast than the published lists assume.

Take purchases from the weekly lists and shape from the lifetime ones. The three weekly sets — 5,528 favourites from Account 393148161, 4,738 from Account 845417474 and 3,134 from Account 354529981 — were revised between 2026-05-03 and 2026-05-21. Two of them publish names in characters this page does not reprint, and the third names a bombing, so it is cited here as build 301176 and nothing more. `actually good geist build` from Account 28487849 at 48,647 favourites and the 209,202-favourite list from Account 394955797 both date from 2024, so read their point order and skip their shop.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Essence Bomb first, unanimously. All five published lists open on it and all five leave Soul Exchange for last, which is as much agreement as this roster ever produces.

The disagreement is in the middle two. Three of the five take Life Drain second and two take Malice second, and the split maps onto how the lane is going. Life Drain second when you are being poked and need the health back on a timer. Malice second when you are the one pressing and want the bomb to land on an amplified target.

Soul Exchange last is not neglect. It is a single decision rather than a rate, so a point in it buys far less than a point in the two abilities you cast every fight.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes beat Lady Geist in RankLock's own matches, and the counter endpoint sharpens the same three. She wins 46.0% of 299,290 matches against Victor and 46.0% of 339,648 against Seven, then 46.9% of 283,630 against Graves; the counter rows read Victor 42.8% over 26,499 games, Graves 44.9% over 22,292 and Ivy 45.4% over 21,240. Kelvin at 47.5% over 133,023 matches and Ivy at 48.1% over 201,647 round out the list.

In those lanes, stop paying health for openings. The kit is a loan you repay with Life Drain, and these are the heroes who either out-range the tether or finish you before it does. Farm safely, hold Soul Exchange for the moment they commit, and let the swap be your whole contribution to the fight.

## Items that go well

Buy survival before the third amplifier. Cheat Death wins 55.2% of 419,718 games on her and Witchmail 55.6% of 360,531, and on a hero who opens fights at reduced health those two are what turn a spent bar into a won fight instead of a corpse.

Echo Shard at 55.0% over 307,904 games is the luxury that earns its slot, because a second Essence Bomb is the only way to double the opening the entire build is arranged around. No ally pairing clears RankLock's reporting floor on her, so there is no partner to plan around; the amplification is hers alone and it works beside anyone who can hold a target still.
