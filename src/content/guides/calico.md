## Who plays Calico, and why

Take Calico when you intend to spend the match somewhere the enemy team is not looking, and take the fight before their carry can afford to punish a 730 HP hero for being close. She wins 51.8% of matches decided under 30 minutes, 51.1% of those running 30 to 45 and 48.7% past 45, a drop of 3.1 points, so the pressure you apply early is the pressure that counts.

Her kit is built for arriving rather than holding. Gloom Bombs drop a delayed cluster, and overlapping bombs land for 65% damage, so spreading the cluster across the ground beats piling it on one spot. Leaping Slash dashes into a circular melee swing and heals when it catches a hero. Ava lets you possess the cat, which takes you off the minimap and ramps up your move speed. Return to Shadows makes you untargetable and does spirit damage both on the way out and on the way back.

## How to play Calico as a Damage carry

Farm the flank, not the lane. At 6.8 m/s, faster still inside the cat, you can be two lanes away between waves, and the two purchases that mark a Calico build buy exactly that. Stalker appears on her 6.6 times more often than across the field and Spirit Strike 6.0 times, one for the chase and one for the burst at the end of it.

Her damage is a sequence, not a burst. Land Leaping Slash for the heal and the opening, drop Gloom Bombs on the ground the target has to walk across, and only then decide whether to stay. Spirit Burn wins 58.8% of the 2,051,777 Calico games it appears in and Escalating Exposure 58.8% of 1,755,971, both of them items that pay a hero who applies damage twice in one exchange rather than once.

## How to play Calico as an Offensive initiator

Pick where you appear from before you pick who you jump. Ava removes you from the enemy minimap, so the value of the cat is entirely in the angle it buys: behind a lane, above a fight, or on the far side of the mid-boss pit. Come in on someone who is already committed to another target, and the fight is over before the rest of the team has turned around.

Return to Shadows is the exit and the reset. You are untargetable while it runs and you damage on both ends, which means the correct use is not escaping a lost fight but stepping out of a stun window and returning to finish. A patient enemy holds their control until you are targetable again, so do not come back into a team that has been waiting for you. The most-favourited weekly build, build 495288 from Account 863482865 at 5,162 weekly favourites, levels Leaping Slash first, and so do four of the five published lists.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Four of the five published builds go Leaping Slash, then Gloom Bombs, then Ava, then Return to Shadows. The exception, build 394605 from Account 1183614423 at 2,503 weekly favourites, opens on Gloom Bombs instead and keeps the rest of the order.

Level Leaping Slash first in almost every game. It is your only source of healing in lane, it is the reason a trade you win leaves you healthier than a trade you avoid, and it doubles as the gap-closer you need later. Take Gloom Bombs first only when the enemy laner refuses to come into slash range and you have to farm and harass from a distance. Ava is third in every published list because the speed matters more than the rank, and Return to Shadows goes in on unlock.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes beat Calico, and all three do it by watching the flank you were counting on. She wins 45.1% of 185,774 matches against Victor, 45.2% of 210,513 against Seven and 45.4% of 209,841 against Graves, and the counter endpoint puts Graves first at 44.1% over 22,376 games, Victor at 44.3% over 20,239 and Seven at 46.3% over 20,673. Kelvin at 46.5% over 103,583 matches and Ivy at 47.2% over 154,373 round out the list.

Play those games by changing your entry, not your build. If the enemy is holding control for your return, come back from a different height or wait a beat past the moment they expect. Send the cat to a lane you have no intention of hitting. Her rewarding rows are Sinclair at 55.4% over 96,733 matches, Venator at 54.8% over 239,667, Bebop at 54.1% over 290,858, Silver at 53.8% over 166,277 and Mina at 53.8% over 216,333. Against those five, take the flank early and keep taking it.

## Items that go well

Spirit Burn and Escalating Exposure carry the middle of the build, then Scourge at 58.7% over 2,326,079 games and Focus Lens at 58.3% over 563,773. Colossus wins 58.9% of 181,259 Calico games and Diviner's Kevlar 59.8% of 82,645, and one of the two belongs in the build the moment you start dying on the way in rather than on the way out.

The weapon items have the best rates on her table and the smallest samples behind them: Frenzy at 60.4% over 24,066 games, Armor Piercing Rounds at 59.4% over 13,432, Silencer at 59.2% over 51,644 and Lucky Shot at 59.1% over 115,905. Treat them as a real second direction rather than a rounding error, and pick one only if your slash is already landing.
