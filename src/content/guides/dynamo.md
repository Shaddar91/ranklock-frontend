## Who plays Dynamo, and why

Take Dynamo when your team wins grouped fights but never manages to have one, and plan on holding a position rather than chasing a kill. He is a slow, heavy body at 880 HP, 6.7 m/s and 3 stamina, and his numbers lean early: 52.7% win rate under 30 minutes, 51.9% from 30 to 45, and 51.0% past 45. Play for the fight you can force before the enemy carries come online.

The kit is one setup button and three ways to survive using it. Kinetic Pulse punches the ground for spirit damage and throws everyone standing on it into the air. Quantum Entanglement blinks him out untargetable for a moment, and upgraded it takes allies along. Rejuvenating Aurora is a channelled area heal he can dash and melee through. Singularity is the reason the other three exist: a spirit-damage field held in his hand that stuns and drags enemies into it.

## How to play Dynamo as a Tank

Pick the ground before the fight and stand on it. Singularity has to be cast from inside the group it catches, so what you are buying is the right to walk into five people and still be there afterwards, not the damage you deal on the way in. The item table agrees: Indomitable wins 57.6% of the 1,632,788 Dynamo games it is bought in, Witchmail 58.4% of 535,145 and Cheat Death 58.1% of 402,270.

Hold the objective, not the chase. Kinetic Pulse is the cheapest way to stall a Walker push, because the knockup lifts a whole group off the ground at once and buys your team the rotation. Spending it on one fleeing enemy is the cast you will want back thirty seconds later.

Quantum Entanglement is a defensive cooldown, not an exit. Blinking out for the instant a stun lands costs you nothing, and with the ally upgrade it pulls a caught teammate out of the same trap. Keep a charge for that and you can front far harder than the health bar suggests.

## How to play Dynamo as a Support

Treat the channel as a position, not a button. Rejuvenating Aurora heals everyone in the area and lets you keep dashing through it, so the real decision is where you stand when it starts: heal behind cover your team can fall back to, and make the enemy choose between walking into the pulse and giving up the kill. Healing Tempo wins 57.5% of 1,448,141 games on the hero and Divine Barrier 57.9% of 233,000, and both extend the window your team gets to fight inside.

Cooldown items are the support build. All three leading weekly lists open on charges and cooldown reduction rather than damage, and the most-favourited of them, `BIG GORDO AP` from Account 339691297 at 23,145 weekly favourites, treats Rapid Recharge as the first real spike. That is the right read of the hero: a second Kinetic Pulse inside one fight beats a bigger first one.

Pair with someone who converts a stationary group. Dynamo wins 52.8% of the 20,163 games he shares with Lash, whose own ultimate lands on exactly the clumped, stunned pile Singularity produces. The 20,350 games he shares with Bebop read 48.4%, under half, so that is not a pairing to plan around.

## The build on the 2026-08-22 Update patch

Dynamo was not named in this patch, and the cooldown core he leans on did not move. Two items in the wider pool did: Restorative Locket dropped from 10% Spirit Resistance to 8% and appears in 3 of the 40 sampled Dynamo builds, and Radiant Regeneration lost heal on cast from 70 to 65 and appears in 1 of them. Neither is worth re-planning around.

Read the recent lists for item order and the old ones for shape only. `DA BIGGEST WAGH` from Account 918074721 at 54,853 favourites was last updated 2026-09-06 and is the freshest set on the hero. `| Gun Gigio Dynamo |` from Account 139182740 sits far higher at 209,089 favourites but was last touched 2024-09-16, several item revisions ago — take its ability order as evidence and leave its purchases alone.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Four of the five published builds agree: Kinetic Pulse, then Rejuvenating Aurora, then Quantum Entanglement, then Singularity. Build 262104 at 23,145 weekly favourites, build 542757 from Account 1078071513 at 7,233, `PULSE KILLAMO 2.0` from Account 149478327 at 6,138 and build 199220 all take that route. Build 1047 is the one exception, moving Quantum Entanglement ahead of the heal.

Read that split as a lane question. Kinetic Pulse first is not negotiable, because it is your wave clear, your knockup and your only pressure in one button. Rejuvenating Aurora second is right when you are being traded down and the heal keeps you in the lane; Quantum Entanglement second is right when you are being visited and need the escape more than the sustain.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes beat Dynamo in RankLock's own matches and all three punish the same thing. He wins 45.9% of 276,309 matches against Victor, 46.3% of 366,214 against Seven and 48.2% of 308,986 against Graves, and the counter endpoint puts them in the same order at Victor 44.6% over 26,970 games, Graves 46.2% over 24,791 and Seven 46.4% over 30,510. Every one of them fights at a range where a body that has to walk in is a body they watch coming.

Play those lanes without opening. Let them commit, then Kinetic Pulse the follow-up instead of the initiator. Kelvin at 48.3% over 169,779 matches and McGinnis at 48.4% over 187,638 sit just behind for the same reason: both own ground you have to cross on foot. The lanes that go your way are Venator at 57.0% over 352,313 matches, Sinclair at 56.5% over 133,319, Bebop at 56.3% over 344,254, Mina at 56.0% over 307,562 and Silver at 55.9% over 246,211. Squeeze those while the curve still favours you.

## Items that go well

Buy the arrival before you buy the damage. Magic Carpet wins 61.8% of the 101,667 Dynamo games it appears in, the strongest figure on the hero with a real sample behind it, and it fixes the problem the whole kit has, which is getting to the fight in time to be useful. Diviner's Kevlar follows at 59.0% over 458,438 games and Spirit Burn at 58.3% over 1,024,401.

Spellslinger reads 59.7% over 12,973 games, the smallest sample in his top ten, so treat it as a lead rather than a rule. The gun half of the build is real and the numbers name it: Crippling Headshot wins 57.7% of 108,318 games, and Dynamo players buy Headhunter 4.2 times and Headshot Booster 3.7 times more often than the roster average. A knocked-up enemy is a stationary head, which is the entire argument for that half of the shop.
