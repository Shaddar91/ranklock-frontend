Pick this build when you want McGinnis's gun to carry the fight and her deployables to hold the ground it fires from. The standard page spends on turrets and healing first; this one moves the souls into Weapon items and treats Mini Turret as the cover that lets the shooter work. The list behind this page, Rey's Fire Rate DPS Gunnis, holds 29,262 favourites and an update date of 2026-08-29, and it puts over half of its items into Weapon while buying no Spirit at all. A second gun list from the same author, 27,155 favourites and updated 2026-08-25, keeps roughly half Weapon with a quarter Spirit, and both are cited here.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with the lane section of the named list: Active Reload and Tesla Bullets first, and the author's own notes say Tesla Bullets can be rushed as the very first buy. Add Bullet Lifesteal for the sustain that keeps you firing through long trades, Burst Fire when you want a pick window, and Enduring Speed so a 6.7 m/s hero with 2 stamina can still reposition between fights.

Through the mid game the list stacks fire rate on fire rate: Heroic Aura, Glass Cannon and Vampiric Burst. Heroic Aura is McGinnis's signature purchase across the roster at six times the typical rate in Weapon tier 3, and it converts your front-line presence into team-wide output. The author's note on Glass Cannon is a discipline check: skip it while your lane is losing. The second list takes a wider mid game instead. Ricochet accelerates its farming, then Toxic Bullets, Bullet Resist Shredder, Inhibitor and Silencer form its core, with Mystic Slow, her signature Spirit tier 2 buy at over four times the typical rate, to make Spectral Wall stickier.

Late, save for the top Weapon slots instead of spreading souls across cheap fillers: Healing Tempo, Capacitor, Lucky Shot and Crippling Headshot close the named list. Healing Tempo posts a 56.8% win rate over 1,658,382 games among this build's core items, and Crippling Headshot sits at 54.6% over 324,108. The jump from mid-game prices into the top tier is where your bank belongs once the lane is won, so spend up before every objective instead of arriving rich and weak.

## Ability order for this build

Level Spectral Wall first, Medicinal Specter second, Mini Turret third, and take Heavy Barrage at every ultimate point. The wall comes first because a gun McGinnis needs enemies slowed inside her effective range more than she needs a turret plinking; erupting it to split the lane also buys the space to keep shooting. Medicinal Specter second patches the sustain that this build's thin Vitality spending cannot.

Mini Turret sits last in the order for a reason: the turret deals spirit damage until it expires, and the named list buys zero Spirit items, so extra turret points gain nothing from your items. The second list simply swaps the first two points, opening Medicinal Specter for a safer lane. Either way, Heavy Barrage is never skipped. The homing volley is your one fight-ending button, and on this page it finishes what the gun started.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run this page when your team already covers healing and needs sustained gun damage, or when the enemy lane wants long trades over quick bursts. McGinnis's deployables mean she never fully stops being a zone hero, so you keep the wall-and-turret utility while adding real per-second output on top.

Whatever you pick, plan to end early. Her win rate runs 52.3% in matches finished under 30 minutes, slips to 50.6% in the 30-to-45-minute band, and falls to 49.7% in games past 45 minutes. A gun lead that turns into Walkers and the urn before the half hour plays to the curve; a gun lead you sit on does nothing.

## Matchups this build changes

RankLock's matchup board is hero-wide and blind to builds, so read it as where gun pressure converts and where it never gets started. Seven is her worst matchup at a 46.3% win share over 232,242 matches, with Victor next at 47.4% over 196,762 and Graves at 48.8% over 315,250. The counter table confirms Graves hardest at a 45.6% win share over 25,674 games. Into those lanes, drop Spectral Wall between yourself and the engage path and let the turret eat attention while you fire from the second line.

Push hardest into Sinclair, where McGinnis holds a 59.4% win share over 86,698 matches, and into Venator at 57.9% over 224,395 and Mina at 57.8% over 198,290. Those are the matchups where an early fire-rate lead snowballs into objectives. Kelvin and Paige sit within two points of even, so treat them as lanes your item timing decides.

## The numbers behind this build

Read the named list as a pure gun page: over half of its items and its souls are Weapon, none are Spirit, and the rest is Vitality for staying alive in the pocket. The second list hedges with about a quarter Spirit, which is where its Mystic Slow and Silencer utility comes from. Both put their faith in sustained fire over ability damage.

Among this build's core items, Healing Tempo leads at a 56.8% win rate over 1,658,382 games, Heroic Aura holds 54.6% across 3,127,792, the largest sample on this page, and Lucky Shot sits at 54.3% over 176,150. Siphon Bullets adds 53.8% over 176,819 and Berserker 52.4% over 315,901. The 2026-08-22 Update never named McGinnis; the only patch movement touching this page is Restorative Locket dropping from 10% to 8% spirit resistance, an item 1 of her 40 sampled builds carried, and Radiant Regeneration's heal on cast dipping from 70 to 65, carried by 5 of the 40.
