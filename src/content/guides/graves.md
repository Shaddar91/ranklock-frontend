## Who plays Graves, and why

Take Graves when your team needs the map to belong to somebody before the halfway mark, and understand that the clock is the enemy she cannot out-farm. She wins 63.6% of matches decided under 30 minutes, 57.4% of those running 30 to 45, and 53.8% past 45 — a 9.9-point slide from her best window to her worst, the shape of a hero who is already ahead and stops gaining. She has 730 HP, moves at 7.0 m/s and holds only 2 stamina, so the pressure has to come from what she leaves on the ground, not from chasing.

Jar of Dead banks every death near her and throws the stored Deadheads out to chase a target. Grasping Hands lays a line that pins whoever walks into it and leaves behind a rift and a Ghoul. Essence Theft turns her weapon into a drain that steals weapon damage and spirit resistance over time. Borrowed Decree plants a gravestone that keeps producing ghouls for a long window. Every one of them pays you for standing somewhere on purpose.

## How to play Graves as a Damage carry

Farm into charges, not into souls. Jar of Dead is the only ability that gets stronger for free, so the camps and waves you clear before a fight are ammunition you carry into it. Arrive with charges banked and the first exchange is decided before anyone shoots.

The spirit line does the scaling and the numbers are blunt about it. Scourge wins 62.9% of the 196,079 Graves games it appears in, Spirit Burn 61.2% of 899,784, and Escalating Exposure 60.9% across 2,779,579 games, the largest sample on the hero. That last figure matters more than its position in the list: it holds up over millions of matches rather than a good week.

Her weapon is a pistol and Essence Theft is what makes it worth firing, so do not skip gun contact in favour of pure ability damage. Every tick of that drain is spirit resistance the enemy no longer has when the ghouls arrive.

## How to play Graves as an Offensive initiator

Open with the ground, not with the body. Grasping Hands is the fight starter: the pin is what turns a scattered enemy into three targets standing in your Deadheads, and the rift and Ghoul it leaves behind keep the area expensive after the stun ends. Cast it into the doorway or the ramp the enemy has to use, then commit.

Borrowed Decree is a zoning ultimate on a long timer, so treat it as an objective tool. Plant it before your team hits a Walker or contests the mid-boss, not after a fight has already gone wrong, and let the ghoul stream do the arguing while your team damages the objective. With 2 stamina you cannot reposition out of a bad cast, which is the real reason to place it early rather than reactively.

Buy Heroic Aura for that plan. Graves players take it 5.2 times more often than the roster average and Restorative Shot 3.7 times, and the pair reads as one idea: keep the summons hitting, keep yourself in the lane long enough to have them.

## The build on the 2026-08-22 Update patch

Graves was not named in this patch. One item she does carry moved: Restorative Locket dropped from 10% Spirit Resistance to 8%, and it appears in 6 of the 40 sampled Graves builds, so those lists are marginally weaker than they read. Radiant Regeneration also lost heal on cast from 70 to 65, and no sampled Graves build carries it at all.

Two of the five published lists are current enough to buy from directly. `YT Farmingbip Spirit Build Annotated` from Account 1063111216 at 32,756 favourites was updated 2026-09-04, and `AndrewChicken's Raging Swarm Build` from Account 95246613 at 30,569 favourites on 2026-08-06. The three weekly lists sit at 11,409, 8,014 and 3,962 favourites but were last touched in May, so read them for point order rather than for the shop.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Four of the five builds put the first point in Jar of Dead. Build 419437 from Account 393148161 at 11,409 weekly favourites, build 433098 from Account 354529981 at 8,014, and both lifetime lists open there; only build 581057 from Account 384583173 at 3,962 weekly favourites starts on Grasping Hands instead. Build 433098's published name carries masked profanity, so it is credited here by number and author rather than reprinted.

After the opener the lists split on the middle pair. Three of them go Grasping Hands second and Essence Theft third; `AndrewChicken's Raging Swarm Build` reverses that. Take Grasping Hands earlier when you intend to start fights and your team can follow the pin, and Essence Theft earlier when you are laning against a gun hero and need the drain to win the trades. Borrowed Decree is last in all five.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Nobody hard-counters Graves; the worst matchups on the hero are still close to even, which is unusual. She wins 48.5% of 456,119 matches against Seven and 48.9% of 329,833 against Victor, and the counter endpoint confirms only those two below half at Victor 48.9% over 26,873 games and Seven 50.5% over 34,384. Kelvin at 50.7% over 173,555, McGinnis at 51.0% over 298,351 and Paige at 51.1% over 345,616 are the next narrowest and all three are on the winning side of the line.

What that means in play is that you lose to time rather than to a pick. Seven and Victor both punish a hero standing still in a known place, which is what her whole kit asks of her, so against them place Grasping Hands to break the approach and refuse the long fight. The lanes to attack are Silver at 59.8% over 304,078 matches, Venator at 59.8% over 420,983, Sinclair at 59.8% over 172,710, Mina at 58.8% over 447,970 and Paradox at 58.3% over 275,835.

## Items that go well

Buy the escape you do not have. Magic Carpet wins 62.6% of the 221,688 Graves games it is bought in, and with 2 stamina it is the item that turns a placed ultimate into a survivable one. Diviner's Kevlar at 61.1% over 170,303 games covers the same weakness from the other side.

After that it is spirit damage in sample order: Spirit Burn 61.2% over 899,784 games, Escalating Exposure 60.9% over 2,779,579, Spiritual Overflow 60.6% over 1,680,831, Silencer 60.6% over 636,896 and Mystic Reverb 60.5% over 339,199. Arctic Blast at 61.0% over 84,845 and Spellslinger at 61.2% over 21,910 read higher per game but on far thinner samples, so buy them for a reason rather than off the table.

Play beside a hero who wants the enemy pinned. Graves wins 58.7% of the 20,672 games she shares with Celeste, 56.8% of 21,387 with Drifter and 56.3% of 21,911 with Infernus, and all three cash in the seconds Grasping Hands buys.
