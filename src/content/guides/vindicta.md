## Who plays Vindicta, and why

Pick Vindicta when enemies keep walking away from your team at low health and nobody can reach them. She converts a firing angle into a finished kill, and she does it about as well at any point in the game: 51.6% of matches decided under 30 minutes, 51.0% of those running 30 to 45 and 50.6% past 45, a slide of 1.0 across the whole curve, so what decides her games is not their length but whether the enemy is separated when they end.

Play her like the body she has, though. She carries 755 HP at 7.9 m/s on 2 stamina, so a second spent hovering is a second she cannot dash out of, and the enemy who watches where you land will get there before your stamina does.

## How to play Vindicta as a Damage carry

Open every fight with Crow Familiar. The crow cuts the target's bullet resist and bleeds them for a share of their current health, which means it takes the biggest bite out of somebody standing at full and it makes every rifle shot after it land harder. Send it before you are seen, not after the enemy has committed.

Then pin the hero you want dead. Stake tethers them to the ground they are standing on, so the target is the hero whose escape is a movement ability rather than a corner, and the moment is when your team already has the fight and needs it closed. Shoot them to roughly half, because Assassinate pays extra under 50% health and a scoped shot into a full-health hero is a scoped shot you wasted.

Take Flight for a firing position, not for a getaway. It grants bonus spirit damage and extends the range your items reach, and it also hangs you in the open above people who can shoot back. Rise to see over the fight, take the shot, and come down behind cover on the same stamina you left with.

Buy what her players buy, in the order they buy it. Long Range shows up in her builds 10.6 times more often than the roster average and Sharpshooter 9.6 times, both bought so the rifle stays lethal at the distance she is already standing at.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Spend the first point on Crow Familiar. Four of her five published lists record an order and every one of them opens there, which matches how the fights actually start: the bleed and the resist cut have to land before your damage does, so the ability that sets them up wants the early points.

Take Flight second and Stake third. Three of those four lists agree, and the fourth, the 2024 lifetime build, swaps them to take Stake earlier. Both readings are defensible and the difference is what you are afraid of. Points in Flight buy you angles and the item range to use them; points in Stake buy you the hero who keeps stepping out of your shots.

Leave Assassinate last on every list, which all five do. The ultimate is an execute rather than a fight-starter, and its damage comes from your position and the target's missing health rather than from the points in it.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes beat Vindicta and both of RankLock's tables name the same one first. She wins 44.3% of 286,847 matches against Graves, 44.6% of 255,751 against Victor and 44.8% of 322,160 against Seven; the counter endpoint puts Graves at 44.1% over 32,655 games, Victor at 45.8% over 24,633 and Paige at 46.8% over 23,995. McGinnis at 45.4% over 145,909 and Kelvin at 45.5% over 132,608 round out the five narrowest.

What that group has in common is that they punish the landing, not the flight. Rise, shoot and drop somewhere they are not watching, and keep a wall between you and Graves in particular before you go below half health, because that is exactly when Assassinate stops being a threat you can trade with.

Look for the other end of the table when you pick her. She wins 55.0% of 122,869 matches against Sinclair, 53.5% of 317,236 against Venator, 53.3% of 175,068 against Pocket, 53.0% of 314,930 against Bebop and 53.0% of 157,095 against Viscous, all heroes who have to close distance to work.

## Items that go well

Buy the headshot chain first. Lucky Shot reads 60.8% across 589,658 games on her, Crippling Headshot 59.9% across 1,283,398 and Weakening Headshot 59.1% across 750,918, three of the largest samples on her page and all of them rewarding the shot she already lines up.

Then buy the sustain that lets her stay in range. Vampiric Burst sits at 58.7% over 1,459,367 games and Blood Tribute at 58.6% over 1,700,241, with Spiritual Overflow at 58.0% over 4,452,460 as the widest-sampled purchase she makes. Unstoppable at 57.8% over 634,796 and Glass Cannon at 57.7% over 608,378 are the late luxuries: one saves the fight you were caught in, the other assumes you never get caught. Frenzy shows 59.9% across 29,980 games and Scourge 58.5% across 20,084, samples far smaller than anything else on the list, so treat both as leads rather than rules.

Pick her beside somebody who holds a target still. She wins 54.7% of the 20,494 games she shares with Mo & Krill, 52.8% of 20,919 with Lash and 50.3% of 20,039 with Billy, and the first two arrive with a stun rather than a request.
