Pick this build when you trust your rifle but want the mid game decided by Stake and Crow Familiar instead of headshot math. The list behind this page puts 0.49 of its items by count into Spirit against 0.18 into Weapon, holds 37,289 favourites and was updated 2026-09-04. You still play Vindicta's sniper loop: take an angle with Flight, send the crow in, rifle the target toward half health, and scope Assassinate once they drop under 50%. The difference is where the souls go, and Flight's bonus spirit damage makes every spell you bought land harder from the air.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with Mystic Burst first, which the list's own annotation ties to Stake, then High-Velocity Rounds, Opening Rounds and Sprint Boots for the lane, with Extra Charge, Extra Spirit and Enchanter's Emblem feeding the spell economy and Trophy Collector cashing the pickoffs this build hunts. Rapid Recharge lands in this window and the annotation calls it a powerspike, so fight when it completes. Healbane belongs in the lane when the enemy sustains through your poke, bought around the Opening Rounds timing.

Mid game is the build's spine: Alchemical Fire, Improved Spirit, Mystic Vulnerability, Duration Extender, Superior Duration and Boundless Spirit. This is where the tether and the crow's current-health bleed start deleting frontliners, and Mystic Slow from the utility row turns a tethered target into a team kill, which is what its annotation buys it for. Rusted Barrel answers a gun-heavy lane, Disarming Hex a carry who lives on the trigger, and Suppressor anyone who out-sustains the poke.

Late, the utility row becomes the build: Tankbuster, which the annotation notes Stake and the crow both trigger, then Compress Cooldown, Superior Cooldown, Transcendent Cooldown, Spirit Burn, Escalating Exposure, Echo Shard and Greater Expansion. Rescue Beam and Divine Barrier sit one row over for the fights where your team needs you alive more than it needs the damage. The jump into these tier-4 slots is the widest soul gap the list asks of you, its version of the spike every expensive page pays, so keep the cheap spirit pieces flowing while you bank and complete a big slot before your team touches the mid-boss.

## Ability order for this build

Level Stake first, Crow Familiar second, Flight third, and take Assassinate at every ultimate point. Stake opens because the whole item plan feeds a tethered target: Mystic Burst, Rapid Recharge and later Tankbuster all pay off an enemy who cannot leave, and the stake pins them where your team can reach.

Crow Familiar second because its bleed scales off the victim's current health, so an early crow into a full-health tank is the biggest hit the spell ever lands, and the bullet-resist cut it leaves behind pays your rifle as well as your spells. Flight levels third of the three; one point already gives the angle, the bonus spirit damage and the extended item range. Assassinate stays the execute, scoped onto whichever target your spells dragged under the halfway mark.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run this page when the enemy front line is fat, because Crow Familiar's bleed scales with current health and high-health targets feed it hardest, and when your draft lacks spirit damage for the tanks who stacked bullet resist. Vindicta's curve is flat and slightly front-loaded, a 51.6% win rate inside 30 minutes, 51.0% in the 30-to-45 band and 50.6% past 45, a 1.0-point fade across the game. That makes this a tempo build: press the lane, convert the first two tiers into urn fights, and do not wait for six slots to start hunting.

Stay on a gun list when the lobby is thin and mobile. The spirit page spends on spells that need a tethered or grouped target, and a draft that never stands still gives Stake nothing to hold. Her hero-wide buying habits are gun-shaped, with Long Range at 10.6 times the roster-typical rate and Sharpshooter at 9.6 times as her signature purchases, so picking this page is a deliberate trade away from the default.

The counter table confirms Graves at a 44.0% win share over 31,513 games, Victor at 45.7% over 24,170 and Paige at 47.1% over 23,579. Into those drafts, hold Flight for the exit instead of the approach and let the crow do your poking until their divers commit.

Her soft rows are Sinclair at a 55.1% win share over 132,919 matches, Venator at 53.6% over 339,599, Pocket at 53.5% over 188,280, and both Bebop and Viscous at 53.0% over 338,823 and 170,144 respectively.
