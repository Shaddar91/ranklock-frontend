Pick this build when your team has damage dealers worth keeping alive and nobody queued to keep them that way. Support McGinnis moves the souls off her gun and into Spirit and ally items: Medicinal Specter becomes the reason your carry survives the fight, and Mini Turret turns into a wave-shove machine more than a damage source. Two lists back this page. The larger holds 42,011 favourites and was updated 2026-08-27, the second 29,537 favourites and 2026-08-29, and both spend about half their souls on Spirit while carrying at least three items that work on allies.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with Extra Charge and Extra Stamina. The first list's notes treat the extra stamina as the lane's turning point, since it plays like a hero with a deeper bar and builds upward into Arcane Surge. Extra Charge stacks turret charges so you can shove Trooper waves without showing yourself. Add Monster Rounds for lane poke and Mystic Expansion for turret placement in awkward spots.

Through the mid game, buy for the team fight you are about to attend, less so the one you are in: Compress Cooldown and Mystic Vulnerability raise turret uptime and turret damage, Enchanter's Emblem adds spirit power with resist and regen on top, and Healbane cuts enemy healing where the draft demands it. Heroic Aura stays her signature slot, bought at six times the roster-typical rate at Weapon tier 3 even on support pages, and the notes on Arcane Surge set the rhythm: trigger it before Heavy Barrage or a maxed Medicinal Specter, because the buff does nothing once they are running.

Late, the lists converge on the same finishers: Healing Tempo, Boundless Spirit, Superior and Transcendent Cooldown, Refresher and Unstoppable. Healing Booster builds into Healing Tempo, so buy the small piece early and grow it. The step from mid-game prices to the top Spirit tier is the build's biggest single soul jump; sit on the cheap utility while you save, then spend the whole bank at once before a siege. Healing Tempo posts a 56.8% win rate over 1,658,382 games among this build's core items, and Boundless Spirit holds 57.1% across 1,938,405.

## Ability order for this build

Level Medicinal Specter early in both lists. The heal is the build's spine, and every point widens the window your carry gets inside a fight. The first list opens Spectral Wall for the slow and the terrain split, the second opens Mini Turret for lane pressure; both take Heavy Barrage at every ultimate point and finish the turret last or third.

Treat the ultimate as a support button here. The first list's notes imbue Mystic Expansion onto Heavy Barrage to stretch its area, pairing it with Mystic Slow so enemies caught by your wall or turrets crawl inside the rocket zone. Drop the barrage onto the fight your team is already taking, because a perfect zone over an empty lane wins nothing.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run this page when your draft already has two damage threats and a front line, because a third gun adds less than a pocket healer who also walls off the retreat. Skip it when your team needs you to be the damage; this build's gun contribution is deliberately thin, with under a fifth of its items in Weapon.

Plan around her clock either way: McGinnis wins 52.3% of matches that end inside 30 minutes, 50.6% in the 30-to-45-minute band, and 49.7% of longer games. A support page amplifies the early lean. Your heals and walls do their best work while the first Guardians are still falling, so press the urn and Walker timings hard.

## Matchups this build changes

RankLock's matchup board is hero-wide, so treat it as a map of where your utility buys the most. Seven is her worst matchup at a 46.3% win share over 232,242 matches, then Victor at 47.4% over 196,762 and Graves at 48.8% over 315,250; the counter table confirms Graves at a 45.6% win share over 25,674 games. Into those lanes, hold Medicinal Specter for the enemy's committed engage and wall off the follow-up behind the engager.

Her best matchups reward the opposite posture. Sinclair falls at a 59.4% win share over 86,698 matches, Venator at 57.9% over 224,395 and Mina at 57.8% over 198,290. Those are lanes where you can spend Specter aggressively and let turret charges shove while you roam to the urn fight. Paige sits at 50.1% over 193,118, close enough that your item timing decides it ahead of the draft.

## The numbers behind this build

Read the two lists as one idea at two speeds: both put roughly half their souls into Spirit, keep Weapon under a fifth, and carry three to four items that affect allies. That is the support clause in packet terms. Ally items first, gun last.

Among this build's core items, Spirit Burn leads at a 59.2% win rate over 759,556 games, Boundless Spirit follows at 57.1% over 1,938,405, and Healing Tempo at 56.8% over 1,658,382. Escalating Exposure holds 56.1% over 3,538,420 games, Mystic Vulnerability posts 54.4% across the page's largest sample of 3,750,930, and Enchanter's Emblem sits at 54.3% over 2,640,381. The 2026-08-22 Update never named McGinnis; the only patch movement touching these lists is Restorative Locket slipping from 10% to 8% spirit resistance, carried by 1 of her 40 sampled builds, and Radiant Regeneration's heal on cast falling from 70 to 65, carried by 5 of the 40.
