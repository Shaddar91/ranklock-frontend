## Who plays Ivy, and why

Take Ivy when one player on your team is clearly going to decide the match, then spend every cooldown you own making that player unkillable. She is strongest early and slides from there — 55.2% win rate under 30 minutes, 54.4% from 30 to 45 and 51.8% past 45 — so the tempo she buys has to be spent, not banked. She carries 755 HP, moves at 7.2 m/s and holds 4 stamina, which is the most mobility in this batch.

Entangling Thorns lays a patch that damages and slows everything standing in its radius. Kudzu Connection tethers an ally so bonuses are shared, healing is replicated onto both of you, and neither of you slows down while shooting. Stone Form turns her to stone, then slams for a stun and damage and heals a share of her maximum health. Air Drop picks an ally up, carries them, and drops them for an explosion that leaves both of you hitting harder. Three of those four are useless without a teammate in range, which is the hero in one sentence.

## How to play Ivy as a Damage carry

Farm with the patch and fight with the gun. Entangling Thorns clears a wave, holds a camp and covers a doorway all with the same cast, and it is the ability every one of the five published builds levels first for that reason. The slow inside the radius is the part that matters in a fight: it is what turns a chase into a kill your team can walk to.

Her purchase pattern is control, not burst. Ivy players buy Mystic Slow 4.2 times more often than the roster average and Mystic Expansion 2.0 times, and both extend the ground the patch owns rather than the damage it deals. From there, the item table pays for spirit damage on a wide area: Spirit Burn wins 61.3% of the 532,626 Ivy games it appears in, Escalating Exposure 60.6% of 1,467,013 and Torment Pulse 59.2% of 855,328.

Kudzu Connection removes the shooting slowdown while it holds, so the gun build is real when you have someone to tether. `Highdocker's Gun Ivy Build` from Account 432810999 at 58,439 favourites, updated 2026-08-17, is the published version of that plan and the freshest list on the hero.

## How to play Ivy as an Offensive initiator

Stone Form is an engage, not a panic button. The slam stuns and heals a share of your maximum health, so the sequence that starts fights is to drop into stone as you arrive, come out on top of the group, and let your team follow the stun in. Held until you are already low, it heals you and does nothing else.

Air Drop is transport with a detonation attached. Carrying an ally onto a roof, behind a line or across a lane is the reposition no other hero offers, and both of you leave the drop hitting harder, so use it to start a fight from an angle they are not watching rather than to rescue someone from one. It is also the one ultimate on this roster that can put a teammate on an objective before the enemy has rotated.

Echo Shard reads 59.8% over 1,319,192 games on her and is the item that lets you cast the opener twice. Buy Ethereal Shift, 60.3% over 15,618 games on a thin sample, only when the enemy specifically punishes the moment you land.

## How to play Ivy as a Support

Choose the tether before the fight, not during it. Kudzu Connection replicates healing onto both of you and shares bonuses, so it belongs on whoever your team is actually playing for — the hero with the item lead, not the hero currently taking damage. Once it is on, your job is range: everything on this hero stops working the moment you and your ally are on opposite sides of a wall.

Buy the items that keep the pair alive rather than the ones that raise your own numbers. Diviner's Kevlar tops her table at 63.2% over 31,976 games, Divine Barrier reads 59.6% over 222,996 and Scourge 61.6% over 124,958. Stone Form's heal scales off your maximum health, so health is damage prevented on two bars, not one.

Play beside Lash where you can. Ivy wins 55.5% of the 20,183 games she shares with him, and his ultimate lands on exactly the slowed, clustered group the patch produces. The 21,455 games she shares with Bebop read 49.8%, just under half, which makes that a pairing to accept rather than to plan.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Four of the five published builds open Entangling Thorns and take Stone Form second: build 376689 at 5,495 weekly favourites, build 491693 from Account 1029314182 at 2,770, build 1257 and `Highdocker's Gun Ivy Build`. Build 383669 from Account 128370798 at 5,024 weekly favourites is the outlier, taking Air Drop first.

The real split is third. Three lists take Kudzu Connection third and the ultimate last; the leading weekly list takes Air Drop third and the tether last. Decide it on your team: tether earlier when there is an obvious carry to attach to and you intend to fight around them from the mid game, and take Air Drop earlier when the map is already contested and the reposition is worth more than the shared bonuses. Entangling Thorns first is not in question in any lane — it is your wave clear and your only self-sufficient damage.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes beat Ivy and all three break the link. She wins 47.4% of 309,994 matches against Seven, 47.8% of 234,234 against Victor and 48.2% of 264,829 against Graves, and the counter endpoint returns the same three at Graves 47.6% over 25,202 games, Victor 47.6% over 27,557 and Seven 49.1% over 29,656. McGinnis at 49.4% over 144,909 matches and Kelvin at 49.5% over 146,514 are next, and both do it by owning the ground your patch wanted.

None of those is a blowout, and the fix in every one is positional. Keep the tethered ally on your side of the cover, cast Entangling Thorns on the approach rather than under yourself, and hold Stone Form for the moment their control lands instead of opening with it into a hero who out-damages you at range. The lanes to attack are Sinclair at 58.8% over 115,452 matches, Venator at 57.4% over 302,311, Bebop at 57.1% over 302,793, Silver at 56.4% over 201,407 and Mina at 56.4% over 278,875 — press those while the early curve still belongs to you.

## Items that go well

Diviner's Kevlar leads at 63.2% over 31,976 games, a smaller sample than most of her table but the clearest fit for a hero who has to survive standing next to somebody else. Scourge follows at 61.6% over 124,958 and Spirit Burn at 61.3% over 532,626.

The deep-sample entries tell you what a normal winning Ivy actually buys: Escalating Exposure 60.6% over 1,467,013 games, Echo Shard 59.8% over 1,319,192, Torment Pulse 59.2% over 855,328 and Spiritual Overflow 59.8% over 636,917. Infuser at 60.4% over 26,958 games is the thinnest evidence in the top ten, so treat it as a lead. Buy the survivability first and the area damage second; her damage only counts for the seconds she and her tether are both still standing.
