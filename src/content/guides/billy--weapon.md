Pick the weapon build when you expect a long game and your team needs you shooting between brawls instead of only swinging the bat. Billy's passive reloads his gun off melee hits, so a gun-leaning build does not give up the brawler loop; it makes every swing refund the bullets that follow. Two published lifetime builds anchor this page, one at 37,662 favourites updated 2026-08-22 and a second at 30,305 favourites updated 2026-09-05, both current for the 2026-08-22 Update patch.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Buy your lane first. Monster Rounds helps you shove waves and later upgrades into Cultist Sacrifice, which the build author calls out for its health, ability range and weapon damage in one slot. Close Quarters and Grit are your trading tools, and Stalker is the first real power spike: the author's note on it says you start doing damage and can look for kills from that point. Battle Vest rounds out the early defence.

Respect the 4.8k spike, because this is where the build's core comes online. The 30,305-favourite build names its mid-game category after the spike and puts Spirit Snatch at the front of it for a reason: Bashdown applies it to everyone in the slam, so one button steals resist from the whole enemy group. Bullet Resist Shredder and Enchanter's Emblem fill the same window, with Enduring Speed and Escalating Resilience behind them.

Spend the late game on gun damage with an answer attached. Silencer is the author's prized pickup here, described as a carry item that punishes spirit heroes across everyone you hit. Siphon Bullets and Scourge keep you shooting through tanky fronts, and Leech marks the point where the author's own note tells you to lean on the trigger and save the bat for reloading. Colossus and Juggernaut answer enemy gun damage, Spellbreaker answers spirit, and Vortex Web sits in the build's own best-item slot as the late active worth a key.

## Ability order for this build

Max Blasted first on both cited builds. The passive restores ammo on melee hits and marks victims as wrecked, and a gun build lives off that loop: swing, reload, shoot. The two builds disagree on what comes second. The 37,662-favourite build takes Rising Ram next for the carry-into-the-air engage, while the 30,305-favourite build takes Bashdown second for earlier slam damage. Either way, put points into Chain Gang whenever they are offered, because the chain that blocks movement abilities is what keeps a caught target inside your gun's range.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run it when you are the carry. Billy scales hard into the middle of the game: a 44.0% win rate in matches under 30 minutes rises to 48.5% in the 30-to-45-minute window and holds at 48.2% past 45 minutes, a 4.3-point climb across RankLock's badge-grouped competitive matches. The weapon build is the version that farms fastest toward that window, since Monster Rounds and Cultist Sacrifice pay you to shove waves and clear camps between fights.

Stay on the standard bruiser build when your team needs pure disruption and already has its ranged damage. The gun version still dives and still chains, but it asks for farm priority, and a Billy starved of souls contributes neither damage nor front line.

## Matchups this build changes

Play the bad matchups at arm's length before you commit the bat. Seven is Billy's worst matchup at a 43.2% win rate over 432,702 matches, with Graves at 43.6% across 416,851 matches and Victor at 44.4% over 452,096 matches right behind. The counter endpoint confirms the pattern: Graves holds Billy to a 40.0% win share over 31,507 games. In these games the weapon build's extra ranged presence lets you contribute damage before you ever have to dive.

Press the good ones. Billy beats Sinclair at a 55.5% win rate over 177,445 matches, Venator at 54.2% across 452,224 matches, and Mina at 53.6% over 356,919 matches. Against the heroes he already beats, the extra gun damage shortens every fight the chain starts.

## The numbers behind this build

Trust the classification because it is mechanical: both cited builds passed the packet's weapon-lean gate, weapon share at 0.33 of item slots against a 0.30 floor with weapon spending at more than 1.5 times the spirit share. The 37,662-favourite build splits its slots 0.333 weapon, 0.121 spirit and 0.545 vitality by count, a gun lean over a bruiser base.

Believe the core because it is measured on Billy himself. Among the build's core items on the item-win-rates endpoint, Silencer holds a 57.6% win rate over 597,671 games, Phantom Strike 55.7% over 759,883 games, Colossus 56.7% across 3,644,997 games, Scourge 56.4% over 2,873,737 games, and Bullet Resist Shredder 50.3% across a massive 4,644,969 games, each from badge-grouped competitive matches with a 10,000-game floor.

Note the patch context before you queue. The 2026-08-22 Update does not name Billy. It does touch one item his builds carry: Restorative Locket, whose Spirit Resistance went from 10% down to 8%, and 8 of his 40 sampled builds carry it. Every win rate on this page covers matches played up to 2026-09-14.
