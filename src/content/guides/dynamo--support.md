Pick the support build when your carry is the win condition and your job is to keep that carry standing through their ultimate window. You spend your souls on auras, heals and team actives, and you start fights with Kinetic Pulse so someone else can finish them. The page follows one published lifetime build at 22,276 favourites, updated 2026-09-06 for the 2026-08-22 Update patch, that passed the packet's support gate by carrying three ally-targeting items.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Buy the lane you are actually having. Monster Rounds goes in when you are getting pushed in, and the author's note names the McGinnis, Rem and Ivy lanes as the cases where you want it; skip it when you are winning the wave. Grit is the trading tool, bought so you can slam into the enemy and shoot through the answer. Extra Regen and Close Quarters cover the chip damage, and Extra Charge keeps a second pulse ready.

Get Heroic Aura early, because it is the item that makes this a support build: your carry shoots harder simply for standing near you. Arcane Surge follows in most games, with the author's caveat that it broadcasts a sound cue when you use it. Spirit Shielding is the author's usual defensive pick, on the reasoning that spirit damage is what actually shreds Dynamo, and Enchanter's Emblem adds resists plus health in one slot.

Rush one big active after your first pick participation. The build's own ordering note calls for Unstoppable or Refresher before the 20-minute mark: Unstoppable when the enemy has the control to stop your channel, Refresher when your team can use a second Singularity. Crossing into the mid-game investment step, sell Monster Rounds and Close Quarters to free slots, then buy Warp Stone, Compress Cooldown, Superior Cooldown, Duration Extender and Mystic Expansion as the game allows. When the game goes late, Greater Expansion, Superior Duration, Transcendent Cooldown, Cheat Death, Healing Tempo and Healing Nova fill the remaining slots.

## Ability order for this build

Max Kinetic Pulse first, Rejuvenating Aurora second, Quantum Entanglement third, and take Singularity whenever points are offered. The pulse leads because the knockup is your lane presence and your peel in one button. Aurora second because the channelled area heal is what your carry is drafting you for, and Dynamo can still dash and melee while it runs, so you never stop fighting to heal. Quantum Entanglement third, but respect what its upgrades do on this build: the teleport that brings allies is how you reposition a caught carry out of a lost fight or into a won one. Singularity on schedule, because the pull, stun and damage over time is the fight your team is waiting for.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run it when the draft already has its damage. Dynamo leans early: a 52.7% win rate in matches under 30 minutes steps down to 51.9% in the 30-to-45-minute window and 51.0% past 45 minutes across RankLock's badge-grouped competitive matches. The support build presses that early edge, wins the lane, and groups before the game slips into the part where Dynamo fades.

Know what you are giving up. The hero's signature purchases across all sampled builds are gun items, Headhunter bought at 4.2 times the all-builds rate and Headshot Booster at 3.7 times. This build deliberately spends those souls on the team instead. Pick it when your carry can convert an aura and a heal into kills; stay on a damage-leaning build when nobody on your side can.

Pair yourself with the right allies. Dynamo wins alongside Lash at a 53.1% win share over 20,697 games together, and with Bebop at 49.1% over 21,060 games.

## Matchups this build changes

Play the hard matchups around their interrupts. Victor is Dynamo's worst matchup at a 45.8% win rate over 291,461 matches, confirmed by the counter endpoint at a 44.4% win share over 27,014 games, with Seven at 46.3% over 381,344 matches and Graves at 48.0% over 326,550 matches on the matchup endpoint. In these games, hold Singularity until the enemy's key interrupt is spent, and let Kinetic Pulse do the peeling while Aurora keeps your carry alive through the focus.

Take the good matchups as licence to group early. Dynamo beats Venator at a 57.1% win rate over 372,496 matches, Sinclair at 56.5% over 141,538 matches, and Bebop at 56.2% over 365,199 matches. A support build that is already ahead wins by standing next to its carry and daring the enemy to walk in.
