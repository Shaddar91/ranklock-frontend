## Who plays Celeste, and why

Play Celeste as a duelist who has to pick one target and delete it, not as a caster who chips at a fight from the back. She has 690 HP, moves at 6.2 m/s and carries 4 stamina, and the win rate falls away as the match runs: 55.1% under 30 minutes, 52.1% from 30 to 45, 49.4% past 45, a slide of 5.8 points. Every minute you spend farming instead of forcing a kill is a minute spent in a worse version of the hero.

Her kit is a chain of marks. Light Eater flares out a cone, and the enemies it marks take extra spirit damage and feed her lifesteal. Radiant Daggers plants a delayed light beam, and each hit stacks her spirit damage higher. Dazzling Trick is a barrier on herself that silences and damages nearby enemies when it breaks. Shining Wonder is a bouncing orb that deals spirit damage, slows, and cuts the dash distance of whoever it clips.

## How to play Celeste as a Damage carry

Mark first, then commit. Light Eater is what turns a normal trade into a winning one, because the mark is both your damage bonus and your healing, and shooting an unmarked target is the most common way to lose a fight you had. Once the mark is on, hold the target in the Radiant Daggers circle so the stacks climb, and only spend Dazzling Trick when you are already taking damage rather than as an opener.

The item table wants a hero who is standing in the fight. Witchmail wins 58.2% of the 1,620,912 Celeste games it appears in, Scourge 58.6% of 501,919, Spirit Burn 56.7% of 737,725, and her single most distinctive purchase is Spellslinger, bought 5.1 times more often on her than across the field and winning 55.6% of 3,717,365 games. Blood Tribute at 59.6% over 66,806 games and Bullet Lifesteal at 57.8% over 256,126 both pay a hero already healing off the mark.

## How to play Celeste as an Offensive initiator

Open the fight yourself, from the flank, at a distance you can cross while the barrier is up. Dazzling Trick makes the entry survivable and the break punishes anyone who stood next to you to kill you, since it silences and damages the moment it goes. That is the sequence: enter with the barrier, flare Light Eater across as many of them as the cone catches, plant the daggers on the ground they retreat over.

Shining Wonder is now a finishing tool rather than an opener. It bounces, so it wants enemies grouped, and it reduces dash distance, which is what actually stops the escape. Do not throw it to start a fight from range and hope the bounces find someone. Two of your three most-favourited weekly lists put Light Eater first, and the two most current lifetime builds do the same, which is the published consensus on where the fight begins.

## The build on the 2026-08-22 Update patch

Celeste is the only hero this patch touched, and ten of its lines are hers. Dazzling Trick went from a 32s cooldown to 34s, its second upgrade now grants +70 of barrier instead of +80, with scaling raised from +0.7 to +0.76, its third upgrade now cuts 20s instead of 18s but grants +1.25s of silence rather than +1.5s. Radiant Daggers lost Spirit Amp per stack, from +8% to +7%, while its third upgrade gained per-stack amp from +3% to +4%. Shining Wonder took the deepest cut: bounce range from 17.5m to 16.5m, damage from 165 to 140, spirit scaling from 0.9 to 0.6, with the second upgrade now adding +0.45 of that scaling back.

Any community build dated before 2026-08-22 over-rates her, so run a date check on the list you are about to copy. All three of her leading weekly builds were last updated in March and April 2026: `data | Pony Killer` from Account 354529981 at 8,581 weekly favourites on 2026-04-04, build 439323 from Account 393148161 at 6,107 on the same day, build 443519 from Account 1183614423 at 6,062 on 2026-03-27. The two lists that have been revised since the patch are `TOP 1 CELESTE NA - SHMUCK` from Account 157629394 at 46,273 favourites, updated 2026-09-05, and `Horn Sounding` from Account 197770830 at 28,436 favourites, updated 2026-09-04. Take your item order from those two.

The patch's item changes reach a large share of her published builds too. Restorative Locket dropped from 10% to 8% Spirit Resistance and sits in 19 of her 40 sampled builds, and Radiant Regeneration lost heal on cast from 70 to 65 and sits in 13, so roughly half the published Celeste lists lost a little of something.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Three of the five published builds go Light Eater, then Dazzling Trick, then Radiant Daggers, then Shining Wonder, including both post-patch lifetime lists. Build 443519 goes Light Eater, then Radiant Daggers, then Dazzling Trick. Build 439323 is the outlier and opens on Radiant Daggers.

Light Eater first is the right default now. The mark is the one part of the kit the patch left alone, it carries your lifesteal, and it is what makes a 690 HP hero able to trade at all. Radiant Daggers first is the greedy lane opener and it lost per-stack amp in this patch, so it is a worse gamble than it was in April. Dazzling Trick is second in four of the five because the barrier is what lets you stay in range long enough for the mark to matter, and Shining Wonder goes in on unlock and is spent on groups.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

The nerfs landed on her reach, which is the part of her game her three worst matchups already contested. She wins 42.4% of 289,674 matches against Victor, 42.8% of 369,646 against Seven and 44.2% of 374,365 against Graves, and the counter endpoint confirms the order at Victor 44.1% over 28,952 games, Graves 45.5% over 39,661 and Seven 46.0% over 34,204. Kelvin at 45.2% over 156,466 matches and Ivy at 45.7% over 237,909 sit just behind.

Against that group, stop trying to reach them and make them come to you. Hold Dazzling Trick for the moment they commit, flare Light Eater into the entry rather than at a retreating target, and take the fight next to your own team. Her best rows are Sinclair at 53.5% over 145,406 matches, Venator at 53.3% over 371,422, Mina at 52.8% over 406,749, Silver at 52.8% over 268,240 and Bebop at 52.3% over 393,925. Those are the games to force before the fight moves out of your range.

## Items that go well

Buy an escape before you buy more damage. Ethereal Shift wins 58.8% of the 569,327 Celeste games it appears in and Magic Carpet 57.9% of 58,171, and both answer the same problem: a thin hero who has to be inside the fight and then somewhere else. Diviner's Kevlar at 56.2% over 34,760 games and Divine Barrier at 55.9% over 52,628 are the buys when the damage coming at you is spirit. Torment Pulse is her other signature purchase at 3.8 times the roster rate, and it does its work while you are holding a target in the daggers.

Her allies pull in a clear direction. Celeste wins 58.7% of the 20,672 games she shares with Graves, 55.2% of the 20,267 with Paige and 52.2% of the 21,366 with Infernus. Graves is also the third hero on her counter list. The hero who ruins your lane is the hero you want holding the ground while you mark someone.
