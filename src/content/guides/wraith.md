## Who plays Wraith, and why

Queue Wraith when your team already has a front line and needs somebody to convert it into kills. She gets better the longer the game runs, and only slightly: 50.1% of matches decided under 30 minutes, 50.7% of those running 30 to 45 and 51.8% past 45, a rise of 1.7 that comes from her items rather than from any single spike.

Position like the health bar you have, not the damage you deal. She holds 730 HP at 7.2 m/s on 3 stamina, so she folds to focused fire the same way every other carry does, and the difference between a good Wraith and a dead one is almost always which side of the fight she started on.

## How to play Wraith as a Damage carry

Turn Full Auto on before the fight starts, not once it is going badly. The fire-rate boost carries bonus spirit damage and lasts a window rather than a moment, so the correct habit is to press it as your team steps forward and to shoot from the outside of the fight the whole time it runs.

Let Card Trick do the rest. Weapon damage summons cards of random suits, each thrown for its own effect, which means her damage grows from shooting steadily rather than from a combo you have to time, and the way to lose that value is to stop firing while you reposition.

Farm for the expensive slots and buy the two her players buy. Tesla Bullets appears in her builds 8.7 times more often than the roster average and Capacitor 7.1 times, and both belong to a mid-game where she should be taking camps and waves between fights instead of looking for duels.

## How to play Wraith as an Offensive initiator

Open the fight by removing one hero, not by walking into five. Telekinesis lifts an enemy and slams them where you choose, so the opener that works is Project Mind to a flank followed immediately by the ultimate on the hero your team cannot fight through, and the opener that gets her killed is the same teleport with nobody following it.

Pick the target before you pick the angle. She is at her best against a hero standing slightly apart from the group, and the slam is worth more when it moves that hero toward your team than when it merely interrupts them.

Keep Project Mind as the way out. It is a teleport to a spot rather than a dash, so it clears walls and floors that nothing else on her clears, and a Wraith who spent it entering a fight she cannot leave has traded her best defensive tool for a few seconds of extra damage.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Start with Card Trick. Four of her five lists record an order and every one of them opens there, ahead of Full Auto second, Project Mind third and the ultimate last, with the fifth list recording no order at all. Unanimity like that is rare on a hero this old, and it tracks what wins her lane, because the cards scale off shooting and shooting is what she is doing anyway.

Take Project Mind third even though it is the ability that saves your life. The points in it buy a shorter wait between teleports rather than a better teleport, and in the early game the mistake is not having a slow escape, it is standing somewhere that needed one.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Two heroes lead both her tables and the second one is the widest sample on this page. She wins 44.0% of 319,198 matches against Victor and 44.3% of 439,687 against Seven, then 45.1% of 355,188 against Graves, 45.6% of 180,457 against McGinnis and 46.0% of 157,899 against Kelvin. The counter endpoint reads Victor 45.1% over 36,328 games, Graves 45.5% over 34,670 and Paige 46.6% over 34,509.

What that group does is punish the teleport rather than the shooting. Each of them can collapse on the spot she arrives at from more than one angle, so against them the rule is to hold Project Mind until their movement is already spent, and to open with the ultimate from a position you were happy to stand in anyway.

Her good matchups are the heroes who have to come to her. She wins 54.2% of 149,168 games against Sinclair, 53.4% of 387,373 against Bebop, 53.2% of 451,796 against Venator, 52.9% of 385,134 against Mina and 52.8% of 279,036 against Shiv.

## Items that go well

Buy the spirit scaling first, because her table is emphatic about it. Spellslinger reads 62.1% across 632,211 games on her, Boundless Spirit 59.2% across 2,703,686 and Spiritual Overflow 57.7% across 5,475,693, the largest sample behind any item she buys, and all three pay the cards rather than the gun.

Hold a slot for insurance and fill it before you need it. Cheat Death sits at 60.8% over 262,581 games and Ethereal Shift at 57.3% over 89,388, both answering the burst that removes a 730 HP carry in one window, while Silencer at 59.8% over 1,742,520 stops the enemy doing the same to you. Escalating Exposure at 59.4% over 387,229 and Crippling Headshot at 59.1% over 858,879 are the damage purchases that keep pace with them, and Healing Tempo at 57.4% over 367,351 is the sustain that lets her hold an angle instead of walking home.

Pick her beside a hero who holds people still. She wins 53.6% of the 23,297 games she shares with Mo & Krill, 53.4% of 22,424 with Lash and 51.9% of 21,981 with Abrams, all three of whom give her the stationary target her damage is built to punish.
