Pick this build when your team needs a body in front and the enemy wins fights with one burst rotation. Bloodletting pushes part of every hit Shiv takes into a debt he can clear with the same button, and this list wraps that trick in Vitality: 0.553 of its items by count sit in the slot, past the share that classifies it as a tank build. The list, published as salty shiv and holding 20,508 favourites with an update on 2026-09-02, still maxes Serrated Knives first. You give up nothing early; you simply stop dying for the trade.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with the core row: Extra Regen, Extra Charge, Mystic Regeneration, Extra Spirit, Mystic Shot, Radiant Regeneration, Healbane, Enchanter's Emblem, Restorative Locket, Torment Pulse and Improved Spirit. Two of those are hero-wide habits, Mystic Regeneration bought at 6.9 times the roster-typical rate at Spirit tier 1 and Radiant Regeneration at 6.5 times at tier 3, so the build's sustain spine is also Shiv's default shopping. Healbane lands early here because a tank Shiv stands in front of the drain heroes longest, and Torment Pulse pays you for doing exactly that.

Through the mid game the list opens up: Kinetic Dash, Express Shot, Enduring Speed and Trophy Collector for tempo, Battle Vest, Spirit Resilience, Healing Booster and Decay when the fight drags, Headhunter if one fed carry needs deleting, and Rapid Recharge for the dash-and-bleed loop. Buy to the lobby in this band; the core row already does the build's job.

Late is where the tank label pays. The defensive shelf carries Juggernaut, Bullet Resilience, Metal Skin, Counterspell and Warp Stone, with Superior Cooldown, Greater Expansion, Spirit Snatch and Ethereal Shift for utility. The expensive end holds Phantom Strike, Unstoppable, Scourge, Witchmail, Indomitable, Plated Armor and Colossus. The step into these tier-4 Vitality slots is the widest soul gap the list asks of you, its version of the spike every expensive page pays, so keep the cheap regen pieces running while you bank and finish a big defensive item before your team starts the mid-boss.

## Ability order for this build

Level Serrated Knives first, Slice and Dice second, Bloodletting third, and take Killing Blow at every ultimate point. That is the list's own order, and it tells you how the tank page still plays. Knives first because the stacking bleed is your lane income: each hit refreshes and deepens it, so a thrown knife confirms souls your Spreadshot cannot reach and keeps a deny threat up while you reload.

Slice and Dice second; the dash damages whatever it crosses and stacks reduced spirit resist on the victims, which pays your bleed and your team's spells equally. Bloodletting levels third even on a tank page, and that is the tell: the items make you hard to kill, the levels keep you dangerous. Bank Rage before you commit, because Killing Blow's leap executes only under its threshold, and a dive fired with an empty Rage bar is how a tank Shiv still dies.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run this page when the enemy's kill window is a single rotation. Shiv's curve is flat, a 48.5% win rate inside 30 minutes, 47.4% in the 30-to-45 band and 48.4% past 45, a 0.1-point sag across the whole game, so this build answers the draft in front of you and buys no scaling. Pick it into burst-heavy drafts that have to kill you twice, once through the deferred pool and once through the Vitality items, and into teams that must stand on an objective while you stand in front of them.

Stay on a damage list when your draft already has a front line and needs a second killer, or when the enemy spreads five threats you cannot body-block. A tank Shiv holds space; he does not chase picks across the map, and a build that gives up chase damage for resists wants fights that come to you.

## Matchups this build changes

Seven tops his bad rows at a 41.1% win share over 327,945 matches, with Graves at 41.6% over 318,459, then McGinnis at 43.4% over 188,844, Victor at 43.6% over 356,528 and Paige at 44.1% over 246,720. The counter table confirms the same names, Graves hardest at a 39.8% win share over 23,941 games, Seven at 42.6% over 22,097 and Victor at 42.9% over 29,741. The card on beating Shiv says to cleanse before the knife stacks compound and to keep pressure up through Bloodletting, because the deferred damage is still owed. Into these drafts, expect exactly that, and answer with the list's own Counterspell, Unstoppable and Metal Skin instead of more damage.

His good rows reward walking forward: Sinclair at a 53.1% win share over 139,429 matches, Venator at 51.8% over 362,270, Bebop at 51.2% over 381,758 and Mina at 51.2% over 366,976. For allies, Lash posts a 49.1% win share next to Shiv over 23,218 games, Wraith 49.0% over 20,820 and Haze 48.1% over 22,526, all above the synergy table's 20,000-game floor, and all partners who want the same close fight you do.

Read that split honestly: the cheap defensive actives are every Shiv's floor, and the expensive Vitality slots are where this build buys its edge.
