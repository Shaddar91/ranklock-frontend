Pick this build when your team drafts a front line worth healing and you want Rem's own spells to close fights instead of only enabling them. You still play her the same way early: nap on your diver with Tag Along, trail Lil Helpers behind your carry, hold Naptime for the fight where the enemy stands grouped. Where this build differs is the wallet. Both cited lists put about half of their item slots into Spirit, 0.51 and 0.50 by count, so Pillow Toss and Naptime land as real damage on top of the sustain. The two lists hold 51,986 favourites with an update on 2026-09-05 and 31,400 favourites updated 2026-08-25, and both level Pillow Toss first.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with the cheap spirit core both lists agree on: Extra Spirit, Extra Charge, Grit, Mystic Expansion, Mystic Burst and Improved Spirit, with Guardian Ward and Mystic Shot alongside, plus Healing Rite and Fortitude when your lane trades heavily. Guardian Ward is already a hero habit, bought at 5.8 times the roster-typical rate at Vitality tier 2, and on this page it is also your diver's insurance while you nap on them. The first list's author labels this block as Rem putting on his jammies, which is the right mental model: you are buying bedtime for the enemy, and the kills come later.

Through the mid game, stack cooldown and conversion with Torment Pulse, Compress Cooldown, Rapid Recharge, Tankbuster, Superior Cooldown and Ballistic Enchantment, and buy Healing Nova in the same window. Rem players pick Healing Nova up at 8.8 times the typical rate at Vitality tier 3, her most signature purchase, and in this build it doubles as the sustain your expensive spirit slots had to skip. Rapid Recharge earns its place here because every landed Pillow Toss shaves your other cooldowns, so more casts mean the next heal comes sooner.

Late, the lists finish with Surge of Power, Boundless Spirit, Escalating Exposure, Mystic Reverb, Healing Tempo, Lightning Scroll and Spirit Burn. The climb from mid-game prices into these tier-4 slots is the widest soul gap the build asks of you, its version of the spike every expensive page pays, so keep buying the cheap pieces while you bank and complete a big slot before the fight that decides the mid-boss. For defenses, the lists stock Witchmail, Spirit Resilience and Spellbreaker into spirit-heavy enemies, Bullet Resilience, Plated Armor and Disarming Hex into gun lobbies, and Juggernaut plus Rebuttal when the punches come for you. Both lists also carry Healbane and Toxic Bullets for drain-heavy drafts, and the author's own note names Victor, Abrams, Billy, Shiv and Lady Geist as the reasons.

## Ability order for this build

Level Pillow Toss first, Tag Along second, Lil Helpers third, and take Naptime at every ultimate point. Both cited lists agree on the full order, so there is no guesswork here. Pillow Toss opens because the whole build leans on it: the knockback shoves divers off your carry, every hit cuts your other cooldowns, and the spirit items you are stacking turn each pillow into real damage on its own.

Tag Along second because the nap's burst heal scales with the ally's missing health, so each point rescues a lower-health target and the regen that follows keeps them standing. Lil Helpers levels last of the three; a single helper already farms boxes for you or trails an ally for resist and move speed. When Naptime is up, play for the angle through a wall: the piercing gaze slows and blocks movement before the sleep lands and the detonation follows. Duration Extender stretches both the sleep and your heals, which is exactly what the first list's annotation buys it for.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Commit when you expect a long night. Rem wins 47.4% of matches that end inside 30 minutes, 47.2% of games in the 30-to-45-minute band and 49.1% of matches past 45 minutes, a 1.7-point climb from early to late that rewards the expensive spirit slots this page banks for. Pick it when your front liner dives and survives at low health, because Tag Along's heal scales off missing health and the build wants an ally who fights at low health and survives it.

Reach for a leaner sustain setup instead when nobody on your team can hold a front line. This build still needs a body to nap on, and its damage items assume the fight lasts long enough for Naptime to cycle twice. If your draft is five fragile heroes, the cooldowns you are buying will never get their second cast.

## Matchups this build changes

Seven is her worst matchup on the board at a 40.7% win share over 424,354 matches, with Victor at 41.3% over 354,495 and Graves at 42.1% over 457,412. The counter table confirms the same three names, Victor hardest at a 40.5% win share over 29,055 games, Graves at 41.0% over 37,254 and Seven at 41.7% over 30,329. Into those drafts, hold Naptime until their engage is spent, because a gaze fired into an untouched dive sleeps nobody who matters, and keep Lil Helpers trailing your front liner for the resist instead of sending it off to farm boxes.

The same three names flip sides as allies: Rem wins 54.3% alongside Victor over 23,310 games, 53.3% with Graves over 20,044 and 53.1% with Seven over 20,322, each above the synergy table's 20,000-game floor. Her softest enemies are Sinclair at a 52.5% win share over 196,982 matches, Venator at 52.2% over 393,969 and Mina at 51.4% over 409,174. When you see them, play forward and let the pillows fly on cooldown.

## The numbers behind this build

Read the two lists as sustain builds with a damage ceiling. Spirit leads both at 0.51 and 0.50 of items by count, Vitality covers roughly a third, and Weapon barely appears at 0.13 and 0.08. That shape says the gun is for confirming soul orbs while the spells do the fighting.

The core items back the ceiling. On the item-win-rates endpoint with a 10,000-game floor, Mystic Reverb posts a 55.8% win rate over 1,525,149 games, Spirit Burn 55.7% over 1,585,241, Escalating Exposure 54.7% over 862,518, Lightning Scroll 54.4% over 534,269 and Witchmail 53.6% over 295,211. The sustain core sits lower but on enormous samples: Healing Tempo at 52.5% over 4,364,073 games, Divine Barrier 52.1% over 2,829,137 and Boundless Spirit 51.8% over 4,998,818. Her signature purchases tell the same story from the buying side, Healing Nova at 8.8 times the typical rate and Guardian Ward at 5.8 times.

The 2026-08-22 Update never named Rem. The one patch movement reaching these lists is Restorative Locket losing spirit resistance from 10% to 8%, an item 9 of her 40 sampled builds carried; Radiant Regeneration's heal drop from 70 to 65 touches only 1 of the 40, and neither list here runs it. Every figure on this page covers badge-grouped competitive matches up to 2026-09-14.
