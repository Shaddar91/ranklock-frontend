## Who plays Holliday, and why

Take Holliday when you want to pick the moment a fight starts, and go in knowing this is a hero you have to play well rather than one that carries a bad game. She wins 45.6% of matches under 30 minutes, 47.1% from 30 to 45 and 47.4% past 45, a climb of only 1.8 points and a losing figure in every band, which means the value has to come from the setup you personally create. She has 780 HP, moves at 8.2 m/s and holds 2 stamina.

Powder Keg drops a barrel that detonates on a delay, stacks burn, and can be shot early to bring the timing forward. Bounce Pad launches any hero who steps on it, yours or theirs, and the landing explodes. Crackshot turns headshots into extra damage plus a fading move-speed penalty on the target. Spirit Lasso pulls one enemy in, stuns them and deals spirit damage, and a Bounce Pad extends the throw. Every one of them is a decision about where a body will be in two seconds.

## How to play Holliday as a Damage carry

Shoot your own barrels. The keg detonating on its own timer is the version the enemy walks away from; the keg you shoot the instant they commit is the one that lands, and that single habit is most of the difference between a Holliday who farms and a Holliday who kills. Aim for the head between kegs, because Crackshot's move-speed penalty is what stops them leaving before the second one.

Buy charges before you buy power. Holliday players take Recharging Rush 4.5 times more often than the roster average and Extra Charge 2.4 times, and both leading lifetime lists open on the same idea: more kegs, sooner. Mystic Reverb is the damage item that fits, at 57.1% over 1,079,679 games, the largest sample in her table.

Her weapon carries the rest. Lucky Shot wins 56.2% of 311,196 games on her and Armor Piercing Rounds 55.2% of 43,681, and Mercurial Magnum sits at 54.4% over 415,389. On a hero with 2 stamina, a weapon that finishes a burned target from where you already stand is worth more than a plan that requires you to close.

## How to play Holliday as an Offensive initiator

Place the pad where the fight is going, not where it is. Bounce Pad launches anyone who touches it, so it is an approach for your team and a trap for theirs, and the explosion on landing means the enemy who steps on it by accident still pays. The strongest use is a pad behind the enemy line that your team walks onto after Spirit Lasso has already pulled someone out of it.

Spirit Lasso is a pick tool with a range extension attached. Lasso the target your team can kill before the rest of them arrive, and use a pad to make the throw longer when the enemy is holding a doorway you cannot walk through. Do not spend it on the biggest health bar in the fight; spend it on the one whose death ends the fight.

Buy the escape that makes an opener survivable. Magic Carpet leads her item table at 57.5% over 97,856 games, Ethereal Shift reads 54.6% over 68,883, and Cheat Death 55.7% over 198,058. All three exist to give a hero with 2 stamina a second chance after committing.

## The build on the 2026-08-22 Update patch

Holliday was not named in this patch, but more than a third of her published builds carry an item it nerfed. Restorative Locket dropped from 10% Spirit Resistance to 8% and appears in 17 of the 40 sampled Holliday builds, so a list carrying it is measurably weaker than it reads. Radiant Regeneration lost heal on cast from 70 to 65 and appears in 2 of the 40.

The two most recent lists are the ones to buy from. `Midknight Holiday` from Account 901696021 at 23,196 favourites was updated 2026-09-02, and build 317832 from Account 1822792925 at 16,665 favourites on 2026-09-07 — the freshest set on the hero; its published name is not reprinted here. The three weekly lists sit at 3,330, 1,993 and 1,882 favourites and were last revised between January and April, so read them for point order.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Four of the five published builds level Powder Keg first, then Crackshot, then Bounce Pad, then Spirit Lasso: build 394617 from Account 1183614423 at 3,330 weekly favourites, build 491000 from Account 1111888764 at 1,993, and both lifetime lists. `Harness web illaryaa` from Account 1228015266 at 1,882 weekly favourites is the exception, opening on Crackshot.

Take Powder Keg first in almost every lane. It is your wave clear, your poke and your only way to contest a hero standing on the wave, and the burn stacks do the work you cannot do with the gun alone. Crackshot first is the answer when the enemy laner is short-ranged enough that you will land headshots freely and want the slow to hold them in the trade. Bounce Pad stays third in every list, and Spirit Lasso is last in all five.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Five heroes beat Holliday badly. She wins 41.8% of 236,866 matches against Seven, 41.9% of 169,769 against Victor, 42.7% of 225,912 against Graves, 43.4% of 98,856 against Kelvin and 43.8% of 112,974 against McGinnis. The counter endpoint names a different worst case: Graves at 40.9% over 20,740 games, then Celeste at 44.9% over 22,655 and Lash at 44.9% over 28,635.

The pattern is the same in every one of them. Her damage needs a target to stay put for the length of a fuse, and all seven either leave, out-range her or fill the ground she wanted to place the keg on. Against them, stop opening. Keep the keg for the moment they have already committed, hold Spirit Lasso as a counter-initiation rather than an engage, and take the farm. The lanes that repay pressure are Sinclair at 53.3% over 96,849 matches, Venator at 52.1% over 233,098, Mina at 51.0% over 222,606, Bebop at 51.0% over 287,264 and Silver at 51.0% over 161,629 — all narrow, none of them a free win.

## Items that go well

Magic Carpet at 57.5% over 97,856 games and Diviner's Kevlar at 57.1% over 91,880 are the two purchases that answer her real problem, which is being caught after a commitment. Mystic Reverb at 57.1% over 1,079,679 games is the damage item with the deepest sample behind it and the one that pays for grouping enemies around a keg.

Divine Barrier reads 55.6% over 72,526 games and Spirit Burn 54.3% over 2,277,670, the widest sample in her table and the lowest win rate in the top ten — a reminder that a common purchase is not automatically her best one. Buy the top of the list first and let the volume items fill the slots that are left.
