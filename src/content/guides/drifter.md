## Who plays Drifter, and why

Pick Drifter when you are willing to hunt one person at a time and to walk away from any fight that has three people in it. He does not need the match to run long, and he does not fall off if it does: 49.6% under 30 minutes, 51.0% from 30 to 45, 50.5% past 45, a spread of less than a point. What changes his win rate is whether the target you chose was standing on their own.

That condition is written into the kit. Bloodscent amplifies your damage against heroes who are isolated, and when one of them dies it banks permanent weapon damage you keep for the rest of the match. Stalker's Mark leaves a bleeding mark and, on a recast, teleports you behind the target. Rend is a close cone of spirit damage that hits harder in melee range. Eternal Night drops darkness over the whole team fight, and everyone blinded by it counts as isolated.

## How to play Drifter as a Damage carry

Farm toward the weapon slots and let Bloodscent do the compounding. His most distinctive purchase is Stalker, bought 7.3 times more often on him than across the field, and Hunter's Aura at 4.0 times is second, so the published lists agree that finding people matters more than out-trading them. Then the table takes over: Frenzy wins 61.2% of the 1,252,803 Drifter games it appears in, Glass Cannon 61.1% of 821,048 and Silencer 60.1% of 3,084,266.

Every banked kill makes the next one cheaper, which is why the first isolated hero you find matters more than the third. Look for the support who backed off to heal, the laner who rotated alone, the enemy taking a camp on your half of the jungle. Mark them, recast to appear behind them so they are running the wrong way, and open Rend at melee range where it does the most.

## How to play Drifter as an Offensive initiator

Eternal Night manufactures the condition your passive wants rather than adding damage to a fight. Blinded enemies count as isolated, so a team fight under the darkness breaks into separate duels you are already winning, and the correct moment to press it is when the enemy team is grouped and your team is ready to spread into them rather than when you need an escape.

Before that, your opener is the teleport. Stalker's Mark puts you behind the target, which is the side their team is not watching and the side their own escape points away from. The published builds mostly delay the passive to get that opener online sooner, and four of the five leading lists go Rend, then Stalker's Mark, then Bloodscent, then Eternal Night, and only the most-favourited weekly build, build 493049 from Account 1123344384 at 10,650 weekly favourites, takes Bloodscent second instead.

## Abilities: what to level and when

Rend is first in all five published builds, weekly and lifetime. After that, four of them go Stalker's Mark, then Bloodscent, then Eternal Night.

Take the standard order unless your lane is safe. Rend first pays every wave, because it clears Troopers and doubles as your damage in any melee exchange. Stalker's Mark second gives you the teleport early, and the teleport is what converts a lane advantage into a kill instead of a shove. Bloodscent second is the greedy line the leading weekly build takes, and it is correct when you already expect to be roaming and finishing kills in other lanes rather than fighting for your own. Eternal Night goes in when it unlocks and gets held for a grouped enemy team.

## Hard counters, and how to play around them

Three heroes deny Drifter the isolated target he needs. He wins 45.2% of 439,559 matches against Seven, 46.0% of 408,240 against Graves and 46.0% of 386,529 against Victor, and the counter endpoint puts them in the same order at Graves 44.4% over 40,956 games, Victor 44.7% over 42,313 and Seven 45.5% over 42,677. Kelvin at 47.0% over 206,881 matches and McGinnis at 47.5% over 224,320 are next.

Play those games by hunting elsewhere. If the enemy team keeps a body next to that hero, take the far lane, take the camps behind it, and come back when the map has spread out again. His best rows are Sinclair at 57.0% over 177,529 matches, Venator at 55.3% over 466,855, Mina at 54.7% over 438,504, Bebop at 54.2% over 469,190 and Silver at 54.1% over 331,075, five lanes where you can look for the first isolate early.

## Items that go well

Frenzy, Glass Cannon and Silencer are the spine and the samples are large enough to trust. Behind them, Lucky Shot wins 59.5% of 2,558,843 Drifter games, Inhibitor 59.2% of 1,707,229, Ricochet 59.9% of 636,570 and Mercurial Magnum 59.4% of 989,592, so the late build has more than one way to finish. Spiritual Overflow at 59.7% over 1,364,115 games and Mystic Reverb at 60.8% over 198,202 are the spirit answers when the lane pushed you that way.

Then keep company that leaves people alone. Drifter wins 56.8% of the 21,387 games he shares with Graves, 56.1% of the 22,680 with Seven and 55.1% of the 23,932 with Paige. The first two are also the heroes that beat him across the lane. Whoever forces the enemy team to spread out is worth more to you than another duellist.
