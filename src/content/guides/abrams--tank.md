Pick the tank build when your team already has its damage dealers and nobody willing to stand in front of them. You give up gun pressure for a Vitality-stacked Abrams who walks in first, eats the parry and the opening stun, and is still holding the objective when the fight runs past its first ultimates. This page follows the most-favourited tank build in the hero's packet, a lifetime community build at 26,108 favourites, updated 2026-09-07 for the 2026-08-22 Update patch.

## What this build buys, tier by tier

Open with sustain, because your lane job is to trade, and the kills can wait. Extra Regen and Melee Lifesteal refund every trade you accept, Close Quarters covers you when the enemy answers your shotgun with melee of their own, and Torment Pulse turns your presence in the wave into chip damage while you confirm your orbs and deny theirs. Sprint Boots come early too: you rotate to fights and break boxes between waves, and the author's own note on the boots says exactly that.

Hold Melee Charge through the 4.8k spike. The build's annotation on it is explicit that you keep it once you commit to the Weapon investment that sets up Point Blank later, so treat it as a permanent slot and let it pay off when the expensive items arrive.

Spend the mid game on time and healing. Duration Extender and Superior Duration stretch everything this build cares about: the drain on Siphon Life, the regen window on Infernal Resilience, the stun on Seismic Impact. Lifestrike and Spirit Snatch stack more healing onto your melee hits, and Point Blank pays you for the point-blank range you already fight at. Warp Stone and Phantom Strike are the mobility actives that put you on the enemy backline when a fight starts without you.

Finish with bulk. Colossus and Juggernaut make you the front line your team is missing, Scourge shreds the enemy's own front line, and Spellbreaker goes in when spirit damage is what kills you. Unstoppable and Indomitable are the active items you press when the enemy spends its control trying to stop you from walking forward.

## Ability order for this build

Put points into Infernal Resilience first, Shoulder Charge second, Siphon Life third, and take Seismic Impact whenever a point is offered. The passive leads because the whole build bets on regen: damage you take converts into temporary healing, so each point multiplies the sustain your items buy. Shoulder Charge comes next because the wall stun is your real opener. Shove a hero into terrain and the fight starts on your terms, with your team free to pile in behind you. Siphon Life takes the leftover basic points; the drain keeps you healed through the long fights this build forces, though your items carry most of that load early. Seismic Impact is both engage and peel: leap onto the enemy backline when your team is ready, or slam the diver standing on top of your carry.

## When to pick this over the standard build

Run it when your draft has shooters and needs a body. Skip it when your team is counting on you to kill somebody yourself.

Abrams improves the longer a match runs: a 48.4% win rate in matches under 30 minutes climbs to 51.0% in the 30-to-45-minute window and 52.0% in matches past 45 minutes, a 3.6-point late lean measured over RankLock's badge-grouped competitive matches. A build that spends on Vitality is built to reach that part of the game. Pick it when the draft says the game goes long and your lane opponent cannot punish a low-damage start. Stay on the standard gun-leaning build when your team needs you to threaten early kills, or when the enemy lane can starve you before the regen engine comes online.

## Matchups this build changes

Accept that Seven is Abrams' worst matchup, a 44.9% win rate across 337,308 matches, and give yourself a job instead of a duel: walk at his team first, absorb the opening burst, and force the fight to happen around your stuns. Graves at a 45.8% win rate over 338,996 matches and Victor at 46.0% over 348,589 matches play out the same way. Your value in these games is starting the fight on your terms, so stop chasing solo kills.

Push your good matchups harder. Abrams beats Sinclair at a 56.3% win rate over 150,909 matches, Venator at 55.6% across 356,448 matches, and Bebop at 54.3% over 405,857 matches. In these games the extra bulk lets you hold the objective after the fight is won, which is where the tank build cashes in.

Respect anti-heal the moment you see it bought. It cuts Siphon Life and the passive regen at the same time, so when it arrives early, shift your value into the wall stun and the area stun and let your team finish what you start.
